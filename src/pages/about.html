PropFirmNerd is a small independent site that collects prop firm rules and prices in one place, checks them against each firm's own pages, and dates every check.

Why this site exists

Prop firm rules are spread across sales pages, help centers, Discord announcements and terms of service, and they change often. A trader comparing three firms ends up with a dozen tabs and no idea which page is current. We do the reading, write it down in a consistent format, and link the exact page each fact came from.

What we are not

We are not a prop firm, a broker or a trading educator, and we do not sell signals or courses. We do not claim to have traded every firm we review. Where we describe a rule, we describe what the firm publishes; where traders report something different from the published rule, we say so and say where the reports come from.

How the site makes money

Some links to prop firms are affiliate links. If you buy an evaluation after clicking one, the firm may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. Commissions never change the facts we publish or the order of our lists, and we list firms we have no deal with. Details are on the affiliate disclosure page.

How we check

Our process is on the methodology page. In short: firm's own pages first, a date on every check, sources listed at the bottom of each review, and corrections made as soon as we learn about them.

Contact

Found an outdated rule or a wrong number? Tell us. Firms can contact us too, but they cannot pay to change a review.
