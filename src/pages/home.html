Before you buy an evaluation

An evaluation is a paid test. Most people who buy one do not pass, and the fee is gone when you fail. Funded accounts at most firms trade simulated money, and the firm pays you a share of simulated profits under its own rules. Firms also close or change rules with little notice: dozens shut down between 2024 and 2026. Read the risks and how prop firms make money before you spend anything.
