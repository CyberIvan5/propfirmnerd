Prop trading calculators

Small tools for the questions that come up in every evaluation. They run in your browser and store nothing.

Evaluation and payouts

  • Drawdown simulatorStatic vs end-of-day vs intraday trailing, on your own numbers.
  • Pass probability calculatorSimulates thousands of evaluations from your win rate and trade size.
  • True cost calculatorFee, rebills, resets and activation, plus the profit you need to break even.
  • Payout calculatorProfit split, tiered splits and payout fees.
