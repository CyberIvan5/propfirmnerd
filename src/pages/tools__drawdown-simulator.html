Reading the result

The sample is three good days and then one bad Day 4. The static limit never comes close. End-of-day trailing has climbed with each close and ends up within $500 of the worst point. Intraday trailing also counted the $1,200 of open profit on Day 3 that was given back before the close, so its limit reached the starting balance and Day 4 breaks it. Change the numbers to match how you trade; if your best and worst points are far apart, intraday trailing will cost you.

Firms differ in the details: some count the limit from the highest closing balance, some from the highest balance including open trades, and some stop trailing once you are up a set amount. The rule for each firm is on its review.
