How it works

Risk per contract is the stop in ticks times the contract's tick value. The number of contracts is your risk divided by that, rounded down. Slippage and commissions are not included, so leave a margin, especially on fast markets and news.

Prop firms also cap how many contracts you may hold, and some scale the cap with your profit. Check the cap on the firm's review; it can be lower than what this calculator allows.
