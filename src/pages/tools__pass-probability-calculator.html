What the numbers usually show

Try halving your average win and loss: the pass rate typically goes up while the days needed grow. Try a 40% win rate with the same sizes: an edge that looks fine on paper often busts more evaluations than it passes. The results change slightly each time because the trades are random; that spread is real too.
