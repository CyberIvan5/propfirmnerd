Who Lucid fits

Futures traders who run their own bots or trade copiers, which most futures firms ban. Lucid's affiliate program is closed to new applicants, and its pricing section does not load from every country, so check the price at checkout.

Drawdown

End-of-day trailing from your highest closing balance, $2,000 on 50K, locking at $50,100.

Daily loss limit

LucidPro has a $1,200 soft daily limit on 50K. On LucidFlex the daily limit is optional at purchase.

Consistency

LucidFlex: 50% in the evaluation, none once funded. LucidPro funded: your largest day must be under 40% of profit in the payout cycle.

Payouts

LucidFlex: after five days of $150+ profit since the last payout, minimum $500, at most 50% of profit and $2,000 per request, five payouts per account. LucidPro: once you have $500+ profit and meet the 40% rule; $2,000 for the first payout, $2,500 after.

Not allowed

  • High-frequency trading and microscalping.
  • Holding positions past 4:45 PM ET.
  • Red-folder news trading on LucidDaily only.
