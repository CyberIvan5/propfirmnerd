Who FXIFY fits

Non-US forex traders who use EAs: FXIFY allows them, including martingale and grid. Its operating entity holds a Mauritius investment dealer licence.

Drawdown

One Phase trails the closed balance by 6% ($3,000 on 50K) and locks at the starting balance. Two Phase uses a static 10% ($5,000). Three Phase uses a static 5% ($2,500).

Daily loss limit

$1,500 on One Phase, $2,000 on Two Phase and $2,500 on Three Phase.

Targets and days

One Phase: $5,000 (10%), at least 5 trading days. Two Phase: at least 4 trading days. Three Phase: 5% in each phase, at least 5 trading days.

Consistency

None on Two Phase.

Payouts

One Phase: the first payout is on demand. Two Phase: every 14 or 30 days. The split is up to 90%.

Bots and holding

FXIFY's homepage says EAs are allowed, including martingale and grid, and that weekend holding is allowed. Check its terms for the limits.

Account limit

One active account per account size, counting challenge and funded accounts. FXIFY does not accept US residents.
