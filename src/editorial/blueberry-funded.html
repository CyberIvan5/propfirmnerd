Who Blueberry Funded fits

Non-US forex traders who want a fixed loss floor. It does not accept residents of the US or Australia, and news trading is restricted on all programs. Its products changed several times in 2026, so the rules depend on when you bought.

Drawdown

All three plans use a static max loss. Prime 2-Step: 10% ($5,000 on 50K). 1-Step: 6% ($3,000). Flex 1-Step: 12% ($6,000), including floating losses.

Daily loss limit

Prime 2-Step: 4% of the starting balance, measured from the higher of balance or equity at the start of the day. 1-Step: 4%, reset at 5:00 PM EST. Flex 1-Step: 3%.

Consistency

None on Prime 2-Step or 1-Step. Flex 1-Step has no consistency rule, but if one trade idea made more than 60% of the profit target, the funded account needs 4 trading days (each closing at least 0.5% up) before the first payout request.

Risk per trade

1-Step funded accounts cap risk per trade idea at 1.5% ($750 on 50K). Flex 1-Step caps it at 1%: the first breach closes your positions, the second closes the account. Prime has no risk-per-trade limit.

Payouts

Every 14 days on all three plans. Prime needs 3 active days of at least 0.5% in each cycle. 1-Step needs at least $100 of realized profit and 3 active days. Flex 1-Step has a minimum payout of 1% of the starting balance ($500), and the first reward comes 14 days after your first funded trade. The split is 80% on Prime and 1-Step, 85% on Flex.

News and holding

The general rule bans opening or closing trades from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after high-impact news. On Prime only new positions are banned in that window; closing is allowed. Flex 1-Step uses a 5-minute window with strikes: profit deducted, then the split cut to 60%, then the account closed. Weekend holding is allowed on every plan except Flex 1-Step.

Not allowed

  • Commercial or third-party EAs. Your own EAs must be disclosed and approved first.
  • Copy trading with anyone else.
