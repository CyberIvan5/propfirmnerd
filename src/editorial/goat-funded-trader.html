Who Goat Funded Trader fits

Forex traders who want static drawdown at a discount and can keep floating losses small: under its Goat Guard rule, a 2% floating loss cuts your split to 50% and a second one closes the account.

Drawdown

Both plans use a static max loss. 2 Step GOAT: 10% of the starting balance ($5,000 on 50K). 1 Step GOAT: 6% ($3,000).

Daily loss limit

$2,000 on 2 Step GOAT and $1,500 on 1 Step GOAT.

Targets

2 Step GOAT: 8% in Phase 1 and 6% in Phase 2. 1 Step GOAT: $5,000 (10%).

Consistency

None on either plan.

Payouts

Every 14 days, with an 80% split (up to 100% as a paid add-on). Goat Funded Trader charges 3% on payouts. Crypto payouts are capped at $3,000 each and Skrill at $5,000; Rise has no cap.

News and holding

Trades opened or closed within 5 minutes of high-impact news can earn at most 1% of the starting balance each; anything above is removed, and the account is not failed. Overnight and weekend holding are allowed, but on funded accounts the profit is removed from trades opened in the last 3 market hours on Friday and closed in the first 3 hours on Monday.

Not allowed

  • Third-party EAs, HFT and gold arbitrage EAs. Only EAs you built yourself.
  • Copying other traders or outside accounts. The GOAT Trade Copier works between your own accounts.
