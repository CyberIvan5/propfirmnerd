Who The5ers fits

Patient forex traders who want static drawdown and a long track record more than fast payouts. Automated trading needs written approval, and copy trading is prohibited.

New or Classic?

High Stakes New costs $249 and asks for 10% then 5%. Classic costs $279 and asks for 8% then 5%. Both have a 4% daily loss and an 8% static max loss on 50K.

Drawdown

High Stakes uses a static max loss of 8% of the starting balance, $4,000 on 50K.

Daily loss limit

$2,000 on 50K.

Targets and days

High Stakes New: 10% in Phase 1 and 5% in Phase 2. High Stakes Classic: 8% and 5%. Both need 3 profitable days in each phase.

Payouts

The first payout comes 14 days after funding, then every 2 weeks. On 50K each payout is capped at $3,000, with a $150 minimum after the split. The split starts at 80% and rises to 100% on the scaling plan. Rise, crypto and bank payouts carry a 3.5% fee; Hub Credits have none.

News and holding

High Stakes forbids new orders from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after high-impact news, and every program bans bracketing pending orders around such news. Overnight and weekend holding are allowed.

Not allowed

  • Automated trading without The5ers' written approval. Third-party EAs, HFT, tick scalping and arbitrage are banned.
  • Copy trading or coordinating trades with other traders.
