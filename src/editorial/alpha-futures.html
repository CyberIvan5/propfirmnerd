Who Alpha Futures fits

Manual intraday traders who are comfortable with a monthly fee until they pass. The Advanced plan drops the consistency rule once you are funded and allows up to $15,000 per payout request. Alpha is a UK company (Companies House 15655643).

Drawdown

End-of-day trailing, $2,000 on 50K Standard and Zero and $1,750 on Advanced, stopping at the starting balance.

Consistency

Standard: 50% in the evaluation, 40% once qualified. Zero: none in the evaluation, 40% once qualified. Advanced: 40% in the evaluation, none once qualified.

Payouts

Up to four requests a month, each after five winning days of $200+, and each request can take at most 50% of profit. On 50K Standard the range is $500 to $3,000 per request.

Not allowed

  • AI, bots and full automation on all accounts.
  • Holding past 4:20 PM ET.
  • Orders within 2 minutes of high-impact news on Standard, Zero and Direct qualified accounts.
