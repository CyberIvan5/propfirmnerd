Who Aqua Funded fits

Forex traders who want a cheap entry and a high split, and who can work with a trailing drawdown on the 1 Step and Instant models. News trading is allowed, but profit made within 5 minutes of red-folder news is capped at 0.5% of the starting balance per payout cycle.

Drawdown

2 Step Standard: static 8% of the starting balance ($4,000 on 50K), including floating losses. 1 Step Standard: trails 6% below the highest balance or equity ($3,000). Instant Funding Pro: trails 6% below the highest equity, including open profit, and never moves down.

Daily loss limit

2 Step Standard: 5% ($2,500), measured from the higher of the previous day's ending balance or equity and reset at 00:00 UTC. 1 Step Standard and Instant Funding Pro: 3% ($1,500).

Consistency

None on the 2 Step and 1 Step Standard. On Instant Funding Pro accounts bought from September 8, 2026, your best day must be under 20% of total profit before you request a reward. It blocks the request but does not fail the account.

Payouts

Rewards come every 14 days, with a $100 minimum and a 3% processing fee on every payout. On 1 Step Standard you need 3 trading days of at least 0.5% profit. On Instant Funding Pro you need 5 such days, and a floating loss reaching 2% of the starting balance ($1,000 on 50K) closes the account permanently. The challenge fee on 2 Step and 1 Step Standard comes back with the fourth payout.

News and holding

For accounts from April 27, 2026, profit from trades opened or closed within 5 minutes of red-folder news or FOMC is capped at 0.5% of the starting balance per payout cycle. Anything above is removed; the account is not failed. Overnight and weekend holding are allowed.

Not allowed

  • Copy trading from anyone else's account. Copiers between your own Aqua Funded accounts are allowed.
