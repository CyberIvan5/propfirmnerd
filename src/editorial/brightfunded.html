Who BrightFunded fits

Forex traders who want a static loss floor (2-Step Bright) or daily payouts (1-Step Daily). The split starts at 80% and rises with its scaling plan. Accounts are deactivated after 30 days without a trade.

Drawdown

2-Step Bright: static 8% of the starting balance ($4,000 on 50K). 1-Step Daily: trails equity in real time by 6% ($3,000) and locks at the starting balance once equity is 6% above it.

Daily loss limit

4% on 2-Step Bright and 3% on 1-Step Daily, both measured from the higher of balance or equity at the daily rollover (23:30 to 23:59 CET).

Consistency

None. BrightFunded's help center says it does not currently enforce a consistency rule.

Trading days and time

2-Step Bright: at least 3 profitable days per phase, each closing with 0.5% net profit or more. 1-Step Daily: no minimum. These rules apply to accounts bought from 16 November 2026; older accounts keep the previous 5-day minimum. There is no time limit, but an account with no trade for 30 days is deactivated.

Payouts

2-Step Bright: first request 14 days after your first funded trade, then every 14 days (every 7 with a paid add-on). 1-Step Daily: a request on any day from the first funded day, with a 1% profit buffer left in the account. There is no minimum amount, but all positions and orders must be closed before you request. Payouts are in euros by bank transfer or in USDC. The split is 80%, 90% with the add-on or after the first scale-up, and 100% from the third scale-up.

News and holding

No news restriction in Phase 1 or Phase 2. On funded accounts no orders may execute from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after significant news on the affected instrument; profits from such trades are deducted, but the account is not failed. Take profits on trades held 48 hours or more are exempt. Overnight and weekend holding are allowed.

Not allowed

  • HFT, tick scalping, grid trading and arbitrage. Bots are not allowed on DXtrade.
  • Copying between different people, including family and friends.
