Who Bulenox fits

Rithmic users who trade news releases. Positions must be flat by 3:59 PM CT, commissions count toward drawdown, and Bulenox's terms say all sales are final.

Drawdown

You choose end-of-day trailing or intraday trailing drawdown when you buy, and the choice is locked for the life of the account. The 50K max loss is $2,500.

Daily loss limit

$1,100 on the 50K end-of-day option.

Position size

Up to 7 minis on 50K.

Consistency

None in the Qualification account.

Holding

All positions must be closed by 3:59 PM CT. Holding overnight is strictly prohibited.

News

Allowed, including CPI, FOMC and NFP, with no blackout windows.

After you pass

Passing leads to a Master account, which carries an activation fee that is not shown on the pricing page. Qualification access lasts 30 days. After 3 successful payouts on active Master accounts, Bulenox may consider you for a live funded account; this is at its discretion.

Not allowed

  • Scalping algorithms, the DTC Protocol Bridge API and automated discretionary trading. Other third-party algorithms need Bulenox's approval.
  • Trade copiers connected through the Rithmic API carry a $100 monthly fee.
