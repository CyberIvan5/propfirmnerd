Who FundingPips fits

Forex and CFD traders who care about the split. Holding through news and over the weekend is allowed in the evaluation; funded accounts restrict news (5 minutes either side) and need a paid add-on to hold overnight. The operating company is registered in the Comoros, with offices in Dubai and Cyprus.

Drawdown

The 2 Step Standard uses a static max loss of 10% of the starting balance, $5,000 on 50K. It never moves.

Daily loss limit

5% ($2,500) on the version we list. A 3% daily loss version is also sold.

Targets and days

8% in Phase 1 and 5% in Phase 2, with at least 3 trading days per phase.

Consistency and payouts

You choose how often you get paid, and it sets your split: weekly at 60%, bi-weekly at 80%, or on demand at 100%. Weekly and bi-weekly have no consistency rule; the on-demand option has a 35% rule.

News and holding

In the evaluation you may hold through news, but deliberately trading news is prohibited. On funded (Master) accounts, profit from trades opened or closed within 5 minutes of restricted news can be deducted. Overnight and weekend holding are allowed in the evaluation; Master accounts close positions daily unless you buy a holding add-on.

Not allowed

  • Third-party EAs that place trades. They may only manage trades or risk; your own automated EA needs proof of ownership.
  • Copying between different users, or into FundingPips from another source.
