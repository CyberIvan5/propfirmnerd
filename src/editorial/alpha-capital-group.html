Who Alpha Capital Group fits

Forex traders who want a static loss floor and can keep risk per position small. Expert advisors are limited to trade-management tools on MT5 and need approval first. Trustpilot currently withholds its rating.

Drawdown

Alpha Pro 8% uses a static max loss of 8% of the starting balance, $4,000 on 50K. Alpha One 10% trails the highest balance by 6% ($3,000) and stops at the starting balance once the account is 6% up. Alpha Direct trails by 5% ($2,500).

Daily loss limit

Alpha Pro 8%: 4% ($2,000), based on balance and reset at 00:00 GMT+3. Alpha One: 4%, measured from the greater of the prior end-of-day balance or equity. Alpha Direct: 3%.

Consistency

None on Alpha Pro with Bi-Weekly payouts. With On-Demand payouts (Pro and One), no single day may be more than 40% of total profit. Alpha Direct uses a 15% best-day rule.

Payouts

You choose the payout option at purchase, and it also sets the price. Bi-Weekly: first request 14 days after your first trade on the Qualified account, then every 14 days, with at least 5 trading days and a $100 minimum. On-Demand: any time once eligible, with a minimum of 2% of gross profit. Payouts are paid within 2 business days. The split is 80%, or 90% with the add-on.

Risk per trade

On 50K Qualified accounts bought after July 21, 2026, risk per asset is capped at 2%, and breaking it is a hard breach. Alpha Direct caps it at 1%.

News and holding

News trading is free during the evaluation. On Qualified accounts you may not open or close trades on the affected instrument from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after a release. Alpha Pro allows weekend holding in Phases 1 and 2 but not on the Qualified account; Alpha One allows it at every stage; Alpha Direct does not allow it.

Not allowed

  • EAs that trade on their own. Only trade-management EAs on MT5, approved in advance.
  • Copying other traders or groups.
