Who Topstep fits

Day traders who close every position by 3:10 PM CT and want an established firm with a published route from simulated to live trading. You choose between the Standard path ($49/month, $149 activation) and the No Activation Fee path ($95/month). New traders trade on TopstepX.

What to know first

The Trading Combine has a consistency target: your best day must be at or below 55% of the profit target. Going over does not fail you; it raises the target. Topstep publishes no changelog, and several rules changed during 2026.

Drawdown

The $2,000 Maximum Loss Limit rises with your end-of-day balance and is monitored in real time. In the Combine it stops rising once it reaches the starting balance. In the Express Funded Account (XFA) it locks once you are $2,000 in profit and is set to $0 after the first payout.

Daily loss limit

Optional, chosen at purchase: $1,000 on 50K. Hitting it flattens you and pauses trading for the day; it is not a rule violation.

Consistency

Combine: best day at or below 55% of the target, or the target rises (best day ÷ 0.55). XFA Standard path: none. XFA Consistency path: best day at most 40% of net profit before a payout.

Payouts

On the Standard XFA path you need five winning days of $150+ net between payouts, and each request is capped at 50% of the balance and $2,000 on 50K. The minimum payout is $125.

Not allowed

  • Trading your full maximum size straight into a scheduled major news event.
  • Holding positions past 3:10 PM CT, overnight or over the weekend.
  • VPS or VPN use; HFT. Automation is allowed only through the TopstepX API and not on live accounts.
