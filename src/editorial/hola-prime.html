Who Hola Prime fits

Traders who want forex and futures under one account. The futures challenge has a 40% consistency rule to pass and a 35% rule on each funded payout. Its futures page says there are no monthly subscriptions, but its help center describes one, so check the billing at checkout.

Drawdown

Forex 2-Step Prime: static 10% ($5,000 on 50K). Forex 1-Step Prime: static 6% ($3,000). Futures 1-Step Prime: end-of-day trailing, $2,000 on 50K, locking at the starting balance once the account is 4% up.

Daily loss limit

Forex 2-Step Prime: 5% of the previous day's closing balance. Forex 1-Step Prime: 3%.

Consistency

Forex: none with Bi-Weekly or Monthly payouts; a 40% rule with On-Demand payouts. Futures: 40% in the challenge and 35% on the funded account, checked before each payout.

Payouts

Forex pays bi-weekly by default (3 profitable days of at least 0.5% in 14 days), or monthly at 95% for a higher fee, or on demand; you choose at purchase and cannot change it. The minimum is $50 by bank or crypto, or $500 via Rise, and every payout has a 2.5% fee. A KYC video interview is required before the first payout. The forex fee is refunded with the 4th payout. Futures pays weekly after 5 profitable days of $150 or more, with a $500 minimum and caps rising from $1,500 to $3,000 per payout on 50K.

Risk per trade

On funded accounts, risk per trade idea is capped at 2% and a stop loss is mandatory on every trade.

News and holding

News trading is allowed in every challenge and on Prime and futures funded accounts. Futures positions must be closed by 3:30 PM CT; there is no overnight or weekend holding.

Not allowed

  • Third-party EAs without the paid EA add-on, which works on MT5 only.
  • Copying from other prop firms or other people.
