Drawdown

Position size

3 minis or 30 micros on 50K.

Consistency

A 40% rule in the evaluation.

Payouts

Daily, with a 90% split. Top One Futures' homepage says traders can move to a live account after 3 payouts.

News

Allowed, according to Top One Futures' homepage.

Cost
