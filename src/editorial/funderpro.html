Who FunderPro fits

Forex traders who want a static max loss and accept an 80% split, or 90% with a paid add-on. One Phase adds a 40% best-day rule in the challenge, and Pro adds a 45% rule in both phases. Trustpilot currently withholds FunderPro's rating, so read recent payout reports before you buy.

Drawdown

All plans use a static max loss. One Phase: 6% ($3,000 on 50K). Classic and Pro: 10% ($5,000).

Daily loss limit

One Phase: 3% of the balance at the start of the trading day (5 PM EST). Classic and Pro: 5% ($2,500).

Consistency

One Phase: in the challenge your best day may be at most 40% of total profit; none once funded. Pro: 45% in both phases, none once funded. Classic: none.

Payouts

One Phase and Classic pay every 14 days, the first 14 days after funding (7 with a paid add-on), once you have at least $100 of profit. The challenge fee is credited back with the first reward. Pro pays weekly at 90% or daily at 60%, chosen at checkout, once the account is at least 1% up, and its fee is not credited back.

News and holding

News trading is allowed in every challenge phase. On funded accounts no trade may be entered within 2 minutes before or after high-impact news, unless you bought the Swing add-on. Weekend holding also needs the Swing add-on; without it, everything must be closed by Friday 16:30 EST or the challenge fails.

Not allowed

  • EAs you do not own, and EAs for tick scalping, rollover scalping or HFT.
  • Copying trades or signals between different people.
