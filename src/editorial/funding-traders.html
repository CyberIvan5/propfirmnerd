Who Funding Traders fits

Forex traders who buy during a promotion and are comfortable checking the rules with support first. The 1-Step has a 50% best-day rule and a trailing max loss; the 2-Step Pro10 uses a static 10% max loss and no consistency rule.

Drawdown

2-Step Pro10: static 10% ($5,000 on 50K). 1-Step: trails 10% below the highest balance ($5,000). Instant Funded: trails the highest balance until the account is 3% in profit, then locks at the starting balance ($3,000 on 50K).

Daily loss limit

2-Step: 5% of the day's starting balance. 1-Step: 3% from the day's starting equity ($1,500). Instant Funded: 3%.

Consistency

None on 2-Step. On 1-Step your best day must be at most 50% of total profit; if you go over, you keep trading until you comply. Instant Funded uses a 15% rule.

Risk per trade

2% of the starting balance per trade idea ($1,000 on 50K) on funded 1-Step and 2-Step accounts, and on the 1-Step challenge. Instant Funded caps it at 1% ($500).

Payouts

1-Step and 2-Step: first payout 21 days after your first funded trade, then every 21 days (14 with an add-on). The minimum is $200 via Rise or $50 in USDT. The evaluation fee is refunded once, with the second payout, for accounts bought from May 8, 2026. If the account fails within 48 hours of a request, the payout is denied. Instant Funded pays on demand once the balance is 3.5% above the start; the first 3% stays in the account.

News

Unrestricted in challenges. On funded 1-Step and 2-Step accounts, trades around high-impact releases may make up at most 30% of total profit. On Instant Funded, opening or closing within 5 minutes of high-impact news ends the account.

Not allowed

  • Trades of 15 seconds or less, and an average hold time under 2 minutes.
  • Off-the-shelf, grid, hedge, martingale and arbitrage bots.
  • Copying other traders or signal services.
