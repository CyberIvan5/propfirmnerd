Who Earn2Trade fits

Futures traders who trade news (it has no news restrictions) and can grow a buffer before withdrawing, so they reach the 80% tier. The evaluation has a 30% consistency rule. Earn2Trade publishes its own numbers: 8.89% of candidates passed in 2025.

Drawdown

End-of-day trailing in the evaluation and on LiveSim accounts, $2,000 on 50K. Live accounts use an intraday trailing drawdown that stops rising at the starting balance.

Daily loss limit

$1,100 on 50K.

Position size

4 contracts or 40 micros on 50K, for subscriptions bought from November 2, 2026.

Consistency

In the evaluation only, no single day may be 30% or more of total profit. Going over it is not a fail; you keep trading. There is no consistency rule on LiveSim or Live accounts.

Payouts

Withdrawals are processed weekly on Wednesdays, with a $100 minimum. For subscriptions from November 2, 2026, you need 5 profitable days of $150 or more before every request. Each withdrawal pays 50% if it is under $2,250 and 80% if it is $2,250 or more. The $139 LiveSim activation fee is deducted from your first withdrawal. LiveSim withdrawals are capped at $3,000 in total on TCP50 and $5,000 on Gauntlet Mini 50K, after which you move to a Live account.

Holding and news

All positions must be closed by 3:50 PM CT. News trading is allowed.

Not allowed

  • Trade copiers, on every program, before and after funding.
