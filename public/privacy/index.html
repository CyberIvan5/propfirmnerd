Privacy policy

Updated October 16, 2026. PropFirmNerd editorial team

We keep data collection small.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It uses cookies and collects data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser. We do not use it to identify you. You can block it with a browser setting or extension, or opt out with Google's opt-out add-on.

Calculators

Our calculators run in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to us.

Affiliate links

When you click a link to a prop firm, the firm may set its own cookies to track that the visit came from us. Their handling of your data is covered by their privacy policy.

Email

If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@propfirmnerd.com.
