End-of-day trailing drawdown

Your loss limit moves up only when a trading day closes at a new high balance. Open profits during the day do not drag it up, so a winning trade you give back intraday does not tighten the limit until the close.

Who Funded Futures Network fits

Rithmic traders who accept payout caps ($1,500 for the first three payouts on 50K) and a strict high-frequency rule: if more than 30% of a session's trades last five seconds or less, the account fails.

Trading rules and fine print

News tradingRestricted: allowed in evaluations; in exhibition, sim-funded and live-funded accounts traders must be flat (no positions or pending orders) from 1 minute before to 1 minute after Tier 1 US events (CPI, FOMC, NFP, GDP); violation = immediate liquidation and forfeited profits
Bots and EAsRestricted: no explicit ban found, but the HFT rule applies to manual and automated trading: no more than 30% of a session's trades may last 5 seconds or less; one violation fails the account and forfeits profits with no appeal
Copy tradingAllowed with limits: max 5 copy-traded accounts at once (max 10 accounts total per user); exceeding 5 makes all copy-traded accounts lose funding eligibility
Overnight and weekendNot allowed: day-trade only; flat with no pending orders by 4:50 PM ET Monday-Friday (auto-close at 4:50 PM ET, not on early-close holidays).

What works

  • STEADY: one-time fee, no activation fee, no daily loss limit, 90/10 sim split
  • EOD trailing drawdown on current products
  • News trading allowed during evaluations
  • No payout fees; ACH, PayPal and wire supported
  • Short restricted-country list (23 countries)

What to watch

  • Trustpilot 3.8 with recent reviews reporting 2-3 week payout delays and denials (Aug-Sep 2026)
  • Strict HFT rule (30% of trades under 5 seconds fails the account, no appeal) and 1-minute news flat rule in funded
  • Per-account payout caps ($1,500 for first 3 payouts at 50K) and $10,000 per-user cap
  • Sim-funded profits forfeited when moving to live (accounts bought after 2026-07-01)
  • Rithmic only; legal entity and address not published in the help center
