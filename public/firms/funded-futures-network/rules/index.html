End-of-day trailing drawdown

Your loss limit moves up only when a trading day closes at a new high balance. Open profits during the day do not drag it up, so a winning trade you give back intraday does not tighten the limit until the close.

Account sizes offered: 25K, 50K, 100K, 150K, 250K.

Drawdown

Both MAX Standard and STEADY use end-of-day trailing drawdown, $2,000 on 50K.

Daily loss limit

$1,250 on MAX Standard.

Consistency

MAX Standard: 40% in the evaluation and on sim funded accounts, none on live. STEADY: 52% in the evaluation, none once funded.

Payouts

MAX Standard processes payouts the same day once you are eligible, at 80/20 on sim funded and 90/10 on live. STEADY pays every 5 winning days of $150 or more, at 90/10. Each payout is capped at $10,000 across all of your accounts.

News

Allowed in evaluations. On exhibition, sim funded and live accounts you must be flat, with no positions or pending orders, from 1 minute before to 1 minute after Tier 1 US events such as CPI, FOMC, NFP and GDP. Breaking it means liquidation and lost profits.

Holding

Day trading only: you must be flat with no pending orders by 4:50 PM ET, Monday to Friday.

Not allowed

  • More than 30% of a session's trades lasting 5 seconds or less. One violation fails the account, with no appeal.
  • More than 5 copy-traded accounts at once.

Other rules

News tradingRestricted: allowed in evaluations; in exhibition, sim-funded and live-funded accounts traders must be flat (no positions or pending orders) from 1 minute before to 1 minute after Tier 1 US events (CPI, FOMC, NFP, GDP); violation = immediate liquidation and forfeited profits
Bots and EAsRestricted: no explicit ban found, but the HFT rule applies to manual and automated trading: no more than 30% of a session's trades may last 5 seconds or less; one violation fails the account and forfeits profits with no appeal
