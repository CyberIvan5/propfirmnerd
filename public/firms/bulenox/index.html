What works

  • No minimum trading days to pass
  • News trading allowed with no blackout windows
  • Wide platform choice via Rithmic (20+ platforms)
  • Low one-time evaluation fee: $175 for the 50K Qualification

What to watch

  • No overnight holding (flat by 3:59 PM CT); evaluation access expires after 30 days
  • Commissions are charged and count toward drawdown
  • Many rules are hidden in collapsed help-center sections; Terms of Use dated July 2021 and state 'all sales are final'
  • Rules changed several times in 2026 (April and August)
  • Passing leads to a Master account that carries an activation fee; the amount is not shown on the pricing page
