Every TradeDay rule explained

What works

  • US-based company (Chicago), founded 2020
  • Quick Pay allows payouts from day one of funding, with no buffer and a $250 minimum
  • No activation fee and no daily loss limit; monthly billing stops once you pass
  • Trustpilot 4.5 from 1,447 reviews
  • Wide platform support across CQG/Tradovate and Rithmic

What to watch

  • Quick Pay pays only 50/50 on profit below $4,000 per account
  • Quick Pay funded accounts use an intraday trailing drawdown even if the evaluation was EOD
  • Quick Pay Funded Sim profit above $10,000 is forfeited at the Live review threshold
  • Monthly subscription: the cost keeps recurring until you pass
  • Intraday only: positions must be flat before the daily close, and the account is flattened around Tier 1 news; the firm's own disclosure gives a 36% evaluation pass rate (Jan to Jun 2026)
