What works

  • Static max loss on all evaluation models (10% on 2-step, 6% on 1-step)
  • No consistency rule on evaluation models
  • Published guarantee: rewards processed within 2 business days or $1,000 added
  • Wide platform choice, with US clients able to use TradeLocker, Match-Trader, Volumetrica
  • Published affiliate rates and a futures sister brand (Goat Funded Futures)

What to watch

  • Trustpilot rating suspended for a guideline breach (fake reviews removed); 40% of 4,344 reviews are 1-star
  • Goat Guard: a 2% floating loss cuts split to 50%; second trigger closes account
  • First two payouts capped (6% / $10,000) and $3,000 daily profit cap when funded
  • 3% processing fee on every payout; refund policy says fees are non-refundable except at 4th payout
  • Frequent rule changes in 2026 (profitable days, daily DD, VPN ban)
