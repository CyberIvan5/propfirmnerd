Who FundingPips fits

Forex and CFD traders who care about the split. News trading and weekend holding are allowed in the evaluation; funded accounts restrict news (5 minutes either side) and need a paid add-on to hold overnight. The operating company is registered in the Comoros, with offices in Dubai and Cyprus.

Trading rules and fine print

News tradingRestricted: in evaluation, holding through news is allowed but deliberately trading news is prohibited. On Master (funded) accounts, profit from trades opened/closed within ±5 min of restricted news may be deducted (trades opened 5h+ before are excluded). Zero Master: no holding/opening/closing within ±10 min of restricted news (account closure)
Bots and EAsRestricted: third-party EAs only as trade/risk managers; fully automated personal EAs allowed with proof of ownership; all EAs prohibited in the Monthly Competition
Copy tradingRestricted: copying between different users and copying into FundingPips from an external source are prohibited; using a FundingPips account as master for an external account is allowed (investor password)
Overnight and weekendEvaluation: overnight and weekend allowed. Master (1 Step Flex, 2 Step Standard, 2 Step Flex, 2 Step Pro): not allowed by default (daily auto-close around 20:45 UTC summer / 21:45 UTC winter) unless a holding add-on is bought. Zero Master: positions must be closed before the weekend, or the account is terminated
PlatformsMT5, cTrader
InstrumentsForex/CFD (simulated); leverage shown as 1:100 on the homepage card
Restricted countriesHelp center: no service to residents of FATF and EU/UN sanctions-list countries, Vietnam and UAE; full list not read. https://help.fundingpips.com/hc/en-us/articles/34505029138449-Trading-Conduct-and-Security-Standards

What works

  • Trader picks the payout schedule, up to 100% split on monthly payouts
  • Evaluation allows overnight/weekend holding and holding through news
  • Personal fully automated EAs allowed with proof of ownership
  • First-purchase discount published by the firm (HELLO, 20%)

What to watch

  • Master accounts close positions daily unless you buy a holding add-on
  • Weekly (Tuesday) payouts pay only 60%
  • Operating entity is registered in the Comoros, with offices in Dubai and Cyprus
  • UAE and Vietnam residents are restricted
