What works

  • Operating entity holds a Mauritius Investment Dealer licence (GB24204066)
  • Fee refunded with the first payout on request for 1, 2 and 3 Phase plans
  • First payout available on demand, low $50 minimum
  • EAs, martingale and grid allowed; weekend holding allowed
  • Choice of 1, 2, 3-phase, instant, Lightning and crypto programs

What to watch

  • Does not accept US residents
  • After the first payout, standard payouts are monthly unless you buy the bi-weekly add-on
  • Headline 90% split requires a paid add-on; base split not stated on program pages
  • Many rules hinge on add-ons and checkout options, so the advertised headline terms vary by configuration
