Alpha Capital Group review

Checked October 26, 2026 against 33 official and public pages. How we check

Alpha Capital Group is a UK-registered forex prop firm and a sister firm of Alpha Futures in the same group. Its 50K Alpha Pro 8% costs $297 with a static 8% max loss, Alpha One costs $277, and Alpha Direct $257. Since July 2026, a 50K funded account closes if you risk more than 2% on one asset, and its terms say all sales are final.

Trading rules and fine print

News tradingRestricted: free during evaluation phases on all plans. Qualified accounts (Pro, One, Three, Direct): no opening or closing trades on the targeted instrument from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after the release; trades opened more than 5 minutes earlier can be held; speeches/conferences restricted for their whole duration plus 5 minutes. Alpha Swing Qualified: news trading allowed, but trades opened within 2 minutes of a release must stay open longer than 2 minutes

What works

  • UK limited company registered since November 2021 (Companies House no. 13719951)
  • Alpha Pro uses a static max loss (6%, 8% or 10%) and offers bi-weekly or on-demand payouts
  • Four platforms (MT5, cTrader, DXtrade, TradeLocker); US residents accepted on DXtrade and TradeLocker
  • Published route to live capital through Alpha Prime

What to watch

  • Trustpilot rating removed for a guidelines breach, with fake reviews removed
  • Default split is 80%; no evaluation fee refund is published ('All sales are final')
  • Pro Qualified accounts cannot hold over the weekend, and Qualified accounts have a ±5-minute news ban
  • Max Risk Rule (2% per asset on 50K since 2026-07-21) and a 40% best-day rule on on-demand payouts
