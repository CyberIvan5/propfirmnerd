Apex Trader Funding review

Checked October 16, 2026 against 26 official and public pages. How we check

Apex lets you pick end-of-day or intraday trailing drawdown on a one-time evaluation with no consistency rule, which makes the evaluation itself simple. The catch is on the funded side: each Performance Account pays out at most six times and then closes.

Who Apex fits

Discretionary futures day traders who want to pass quickly. There is no minimum number of trading days and no consistency rule in the evaluation, so a strong week can be enough. Bots and automation are not allowed, and positions must be flat before the daily close.

What changed in 2026

On March 1, 2026 Apex replaced its monthly evaluations with one-time EOD and Intraday products that give 30 days of access and no resets. The old monthly product came back for a limited time as "Legacy". Our review uses the new products.

Every Apex Trader Funding rule explained

What works

  • One-time evaluation fee with no recurring subscription and no reset fees on the new products.
  • 100% of approved PA payouts go to the trader.
  • No consistency rule and no minimum trading days during the evaluation; it can be passed in one day.
  • Choice of EOD or intraday trailing drawdown at every size.
  • Firm publishes a 90%-off code (SAVENOW) on its own site.

What to watch

  • Each PA is capped at 6 payouts (max $1,500-$3,000 per request on 50K), after which it closes and a new evaluation is needed.
  • Automation/algorithms are not allowed.
  • Evaluation access expires after 30 calendar days with no extensions.
  • List prices for the new evaluations and PA activation fees are not published in the help center; they show only in the site's product selector/checkout.
  • Restricted-country list is long (about 90 countries, including Serbia and Turkey).

Questions traders ask

Does Apex still have monthly subscriptions?

Not for its main products. Since March 1, 2026 Apex sells one-time EOD and Intraday evaluations with 30 days of access. A monthly "Legacy" product returned as a limited-time offer.

How many payouts can I take from an Apex account?

Six per Performance Account. After the sixth payout the account closes and you need a new evaluation.

What drawdown does Apex Trader Funding use?

Apex Trader Funding's main 50K plan uses depends on plan drawdown with a max loss of $2,000, as of October 16, 2026.

Does Apex Trader Funding accept US traders?

Yes, according to Apex Trader Funding's own pages as of October 16, 2026.
