Who The5ers fits

Patient forex traders who want static drawdown and a long track record more than fast payouts. Automated trading needs written approval, and copy trading is prohibited.

New or Classic?

High Stakes New costs $249 and asks for 10% then 5%. Classic costs $279 and asks for 8% then 5%. Both have a 4% daily loss and an 8% static max loss on 50K.

What works

  • Long track record: founded 2016
  • Trustpilot 4.7 from 39,790 reviews (seen 2026-10-16)
  • High Stakes max loss is static (8% on 50K), not trailing
  • Unlimited time to pass; scaling plan to $500K with split rising to 100%
  • 70% fee refund with the first High Stakes payout

What to watch

  • High Stakes payouts capped per request ($3,000 on 50K) and only every 14 days
  • 3.5% commission on Rise, crypto and bank withdrawals
  • EAs need prior written approval; copy trading prohibited
  • Hyper Growth and Bootcamp start at lower splits (75% / 50%)
  • Several overlapping programs and plan versions (High Stakes New and Classic, Hyper Growth, Bootcamp)
