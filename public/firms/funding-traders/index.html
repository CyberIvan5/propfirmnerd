Checked October 19, 2026 against 22 official and public pages. How we check

Who Funding Traders fits

Forex traders who buy during a promotion and are comfortable checking the rules with support first. The 1-Step has a 50% best-day rule and a trailing max loss; the 2-Step Pro10 uses a static 10% max loss and no consistency rule.

Trading rules and fine print

News tradingRestricted: challenge phases unrestricted. 1-Step/2-Step funded accounts: trades opened, held or closed within 5 minutes of a high-impact release count as news trades and may make up at most 30% of total profit (payout delayed until below; trades opened 5+ hours before the release are exempt). Instant Funded: no opening or closing within 5 minutes before/after high-impact news (termination); a News Holding add-on allows holding through events

What works

  • 2-Step Pro10 uses a static 10% max loss and a 5% daily limit, with no consistency rule
  • 1-Step pays 90% by default; Instant Funded pays 100% under the October 2026 rules
  • Wide size range from $5K to $400K (Instant) and two platforms (MT5, TradeLocker)
  • Published Payout Guarantee: $500 cash + $500 credit if a payout is not processed within 2 business days

What to watch

  • Trustpilot rating removed for a guidelines breach, with fake reviews removed
  • Evaluation fee is refunded only with the second payout (accounts from 2026-05-08) and payouts start 21 days after the first funded trade
  • 2-Step default split is 80%; higher splits and faster payouts cost extra add-ons
  • Official pages contradict each other on several rules (Instant split/payout cycle, 1-Step min days, restricted countries, news window)
  • 1-Step uses a trailing 10% max loss; Instant uses a 6% trailing max loss, a 15% consistency rule and 1% max risk per trade
