End-of-day trailing drawdown

Your loss limit moves up only when a trading day closes at a new high balance. Open profits during the day do not drag it up, so a winning trade you give back intraday does not tighten the limit until the close.

Account sizes offered: 25K, 50K, 100K, 150K.

Drawdown

Fundamental and Premium use end-of-day trailing drawdown, $2,500 on 50K. Express to Live uses a static $650 that never trails.

Position size

10 minis or 100 micros on Fundamental and Premium; 5 minis or 50 micros on Express to Live.

Consistency

None in the Fundamental and Premium evaluations, and a 30% rule on their funded accounts. None on Express to Live.

Trading days

At least 3 days on Fundamental and 1 on Premium.

Payouts

Fundamental pays every 10 trading days at 80/20. Premium pays every 5 days, starting at 75% and rising to 100% by the fifth payout. Each payout cycle is capped at $2,000, with a $500 minimum. Express to Live pays a fixed bonus when you hit the target, then moves you to a live account.

News and holding

News trading is allowed on every account at every stage. Premium allows overnight and weekend holding; Fundamental and Express to Live do not, and failing to close before the cutoff disqualifies the account.

Not allowed

  • Robots, fully automated algorithms and HFT on Fundamental and Premium. Semi-automated tools are allowed if you monitor them.
