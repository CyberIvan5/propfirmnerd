End-of-day trailing drawdown

Your loss limit moves up only when a trading day closes at a new high balance. Open profits during the day do not drag it up, so a winning trade you give back intraday does not tighten the limit until the close.

What works

  • News trading allowed on all accounts at every stage
  • No daily loss limit on Fundamental, Premium or E2L
  • E2L has no consistency rule, no minimum days, and an automatic route to a LIVE account cleared by Dorman Trading
  • Premium allows overnight and weekend holding and reaches a 100% split from the 5th payout
  • US residents are not on the restricted list

What to watch

  • Trustpilot rating is withheld for a guidelines breach and Trustpilot says it removed fake reviews; about 34% of the 339 reviews are 1-star, many alleging denied payouts (unverified)
  • Long restricted-country list, including most of the Balkans (Serbia, Croatia, Bosnia, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Slovenia, Bulgaria, Romania)
  • E2L static drawdown is small: $650 on 50K
  • Fundamental/Premium payouts are capped at $2,000 per period (50K), and Fundamental pays only every 10 trading days
  • Some official pages contradict each other: the 30% rule is worded as 'total balance' vs 'total profit', and the E2L activation fee and path-to-live thresholds differ between pages
