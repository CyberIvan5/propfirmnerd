Blueberry Funded review

Checked October 19, 2026 against 33 official and public pages. How we check

Blueberry Funded sells three 50K challenges, all with a static max loss: 1-Step at $302.50, Prime at $406 and Flex 1-Step at $490. Flex pays 85% and the others 80%. In August 2026 its help center said some payouts were taking 7 to 14 business days during a payment system upgrade.

Who Blueberry Funded fits

Non-US forex traders who want a fixed loss floor. It does not accept residents of the US or Australia, and news trading is restricted on all programs. Its products changed several times in 2026, so the rules depend on when you bought.

Trading rules and fine print

News tradingRestricted: general rule (all evaluation and funded accounts) bans opening or closing trades from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after high-impact (red folder) news; stop-loss hits are allowed if the position was opened 6+ hours before. Prime: no new positions within 2 minutes of scheduled high-impact news; closing allowed. Flex 1-Step: 5-minute window with a strike system (profit deduction, split cut to 60%, then closure)
Bots and EAsRestricted: only proprietary EAs that encode your own strategy and are disclosed to and approved by the firm before use; commercial/third-party EAs not allowed. Synthetic plans bought from 8 Oct 2026 allow EAs under the same conditions; older synthetic accounts do not

What works

  • Static max loss on Flex 1-Step (12%), Prime (10%) and 1-Step (6%)
  • Flex 1-Step pays 85% and has no minimum trading days to pass and no time limit
  • Prime has no consistency rule, no lot size limit and no risk-per-trade cap
  • Official 30% codes on the homepage bring the 50K Flex to $343 and 50K Prime to $284.20
  • Backed by the Blueberry Markets broker group; rewards can be moved to a Blueberry Markets account

What to watch

  • Trustpilot shows no score: rating unavailable for a guideline breach and fake reviews removed (1,752 reviews, 35% one-star)
  • Help center says some rewards take up to 7-14 business days during a payment infrastructure upgrade (notice dated 18 Aug 2026)
  • Not available to US or Australian residents
  • Prime and 1-Step pay 80%; news trading restricted on all programs, and Flex uses a strike system that can cut the split to 60%
  • Frequent rule and product changes in 2026, with different rules by purchase date
