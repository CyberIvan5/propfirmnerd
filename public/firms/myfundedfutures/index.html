End-of-day trailing drawdown

Your loss limit moves up only when a trading day closes at a new high balance. Open profits during the day do not drag it up, so a winning trade you give back intraday does not tighten the limit until the close.

Trading rules and fine print

News tradingRestricted: positions and orders must be flat from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after Tier 1 releases (FOMC, FOMC minutes, NFP, CPI; EIA for energy, ag reports for ags) on Rapid and Pro sim-funded accounts; allowed in all evaluations and on Builder
Bots and EAsRestricted: automated strategies allowed if tailored to the trader's own settings and not exploiting sim fills; HFT not allowed; live automated trading must follow CME guidelines
Copy tradingAllowed on all account types for the trader's own accounts (Tradesyncer preferred, Tradovate group copier OK); copying other traders or sharing devices is prohibited
Overnight and weekendNot allowed: open positions are auto-closed at 4:10 PM ET on regular trading days (on holiday early closes there is no auto-liquidation and the trader must flatten).

Every MyFundedFutures rule explained

What works

  • No activation fee and one-time evaluation fee (no monthly renewal) since 2026-08-25
  • Rapid plans: 90/10 split and daily payouts after a $2,100 buffer (50K)
  • No daily loss limit on Rapid and Pro
  • Pro allows up to $100,000 total sim-funded payouts per user
  • Trustpilot 4.9 from 22,373 reviews (2026-10-16)

What to watch

  • Rapid sim-funded drawdown is intraday trailing (from equity high), stricter than EOD
  • No overnight holding: positions auto-closed 4:10 PM ET
  • Tier 1 news trading banned on Rapid and Pro sim-funded accounts
  • Long restricted-country list (90 entries, including Serbia, Turkey, UAE, Ukraine)
  • Several metals/energy contracts (SI, HG, PL, NG, QG) temporarily restricted; plan pages show inconsistent figures in places
