End-of-day trailing drawdown

Account sizes offered: 25K, 50K, 100K, 150K.

Drawdown

All plans have a $2,000 maximum on 50K (Builder also offers $1,500). Builder, Rapid EOD and Pro trail at the end of the day. Rapid trails at the end of the day in the evaluation and intraday once funded.

Consistency

Builder has a 50% rule on the sim funded account. Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro have none.

Payouts

Rapid and Rapid EOD pay daily at 90/10. Builder pays every 48 hours and Pro every two weeks, both at 80/20.

Not allowed

  • Holding positions past 4:10 PM ET.
  • Tier 1 news trading on Rapid and Pro funded accounts.
  • Some metals and energy contracts, temporarily (silver, copper, platinum, natural gas).

Other rules

News tradingRestricted: positions and orders must be flat from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after Tier 1 releases (FOMC, FOMC minutes, NFP, CPI; EIA for energy, ag reports for ags) on Rapid and Pro sim-funded accounts; allowed in all evaluations and on Builder
Bots and EAsRestricted: automated strategies allowed if tailored to the trader's own settings and not exploiting sim fills; HFT not allowed; live automated trading must follow CME guidelines
Copy tradingAllowed on all account types for the trader's own accounts (Tradesyncer preferred, Tradovate group copier OK); copying other traders or sharing devices is prohibited
Overnight and weekendNot allowed: open positions are auto-closed at 4:10 PM ET on regular trading days (on holiday early closes there is no auto-liquidation and the trader must flatten).
PlatformsTradovate, TradingView, NinjaTrader, Quantower, DeepCharts, Volumetrica, ATAS, Hyperprop
InstrumentsCME Group futures: CME (equity index E-mini/micro, FX, Micro Bitcoin/Ether, livestock), CBOT (grains, Dow), NYMEX (energy), COMEX (metals). SI, HG, PL, NG, QG temporarily restricted as of 2026-10-16.
