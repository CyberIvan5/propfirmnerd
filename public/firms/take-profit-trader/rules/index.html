End-of-day trailing drawdown

Your loss limit moves up only when a trading day closes at a new high balance. Open profits during the day do not drag it up, so a winning trade you give back intraday does not tighten the limit until the close.

Drawdown

Test: $2,000 end-of-day trailing. PRO: intraday trailing, so open profit you give back still tightens the limit.

Daily loss limit

Take Profit Trader's homepage lists the daily loss limit as removed.

Consistency

In the Test, your best day must be under 50% of total net profit.

Payouts

From day one in PRO, on profit above the buffer: the balance must be above $52,000 on 50K. PRO splits 80/20; PRO+ (a live-market account) splits 90/10.

Not allowed

  • Automated or bot trading.
  • Holding positions through major news (FOMC, NFP, CPI) in PRO.
  • Counter positions.

Other rules

News tradingRestricted: in PRO accounts you must be flat (no positions or open orders) from one minute before to one minute after prohibited events, including FOMC statements, NFP and CPI
