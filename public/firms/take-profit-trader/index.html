Every Take Profit Trader rule explained

What works

  • No recurring fee after passing; the PRO activation fee is a one-time $130, waived with the firm's own NOFEE40 code.
  • Withdrawals can start from day one in PRO once the $52,000 buffer is reached (50K).
  • Test has no time limit and uses an EOD trailing drawdown.
  • PRO+ is a live-market account with a 90/10 split and no buffer requirement.

What to watch

  • PRO (sim funded) split is 80/20, lower than many competitors' 90/10.
  • PRO uses intraday trailing drawdown (stricter than the Test's EOD drawdown).
  • No automated or bot trading of any kind.
  • Must be flat around major news (FOMC, NFP, CPI) in PRO.
  • Test is a monthly subscription until passed.
