Hola Prime review

Checked October 19, 2026 against 23 official and public pages. How we check

Hola Prime sells both forex and futures challenges. A 50K forex 2-Step costs $319 with a static 10% max loss, and a 50K futures 1-Step costs $159 with a $2,000 end-of-day trailing drawdown. You pick the forex payout cycle at purchase: 80% bi-weekly, or 95% monthly for a 20% higher price.

Who Hola Prime fits

Traders who want forex and futures under one account. The futures challenge has a 40% consistency rule to pass and a 35% rule on each funded payout. Its futures page says there are no monthly subscriptions, but its help center describes one, so check the billing at checkout.

What works

  • Offers both forex/CFD (MT5, cTrader, Match-Trader, TradeLocker) and CME futures (Tradovate, NinjaTrader, WealthCharts) from one account
  • Forex Prime challenges use a static max loss (10% on 2-Step, 6% on 1-Step) and allow news trading and weekend holding
  • Forex challenge fee is refunded with the 4th payout; payouts are processed within 1 hour once the review (up to 24 business hours) is done
  • Futures 1-Step Prime has no daily loss limit, a 90% split and weekly payouts; a published invite-only live-capital route exists for futures
  • Accepts US clients; Serbia is not on the restricted list

What to watch

  • Trustpilot removed about 1,300 fake reviews from its profile in March 2025; the firm only launched in late 2024
  • Funded accounts have a 2% max risk per trade idea with a mandatory stop loss; the forex split is 80% unless you pay 20% more for the monthly 95% cycle
  • Futures payouts are capped ($1,500 first payout on 50K) and need 5 profitable days of $150+ plus a 35% consistency score each time
  • Futures billing is described as a monthly subscription in the help center but as 'no monthly subscriptions' on the landing page
  • Terms include a non-disparagement clause and say support correspondence may not be shared publicly without consent
