BrightFunded review

Checked October 19, 2026 against 39 official and public pages. How we check

BrightFunded prices its challenges in euros: €277 for the 50K 2-Step Bright, and €297 each for 2-Step Classic and 1-Step. The 2-Step plans use a static max loss and have no consistency rule. The first payout comes 30 days after your first funded trade, later than most firms.

Who BrightFunded fits

Forex traders who are happy to wait for the first payout in exchange for a static loss floor. The split starts at 80% and rises with its scaling plan. Accounts are deactivated after 30 days without a trade.

What works

  • Static max loss on both 2-Step plans (8% Bright, 10% Classic) and no consistency rule
  • No time limit and no news restriction during the evaluation phases
  • Fast processing: payouts processed within 1 day, no minimum payout amount
  • Scaling Plan of +30% every 4 months with no stated cap, and 100% split from the 3rd scale-up
  • Serbia is not restricted, for trading or for the affiliate program

What to watch

  • Trustpilot shows a warning: rating unavailable due to a breach of guidelines, and fake reviews were removed
  • Default split is 80%; 90% needs a paid add-on or a scale-up
  • First payout only 30 days after the first funded trade, then every 14 days unless a weekly add-on is bought
  • 1-Step uses an intraday trailing 6% max loss with a 3% daily limit
  • 30-day inactivity rule deactivates accounts; US traders are limited to DXtrade (no MT5/cTrader)
