Checked October 19, 2026 against 28 official and public pages. How we check

Who Earn2Trade fits

Futures traders who trade news (it has no news restrictions) and can grow a buffer before withdrawing, so they reach the 80% tier. The evaluation has a 30% consistency rule. Earn2Trade publishes its own numbers: 8.89% of candidates passed in 2025.

What works

  • In business since 2016; Trustpilot 4.5 from 5,026 reviews
  • Option to trade a real Live brokerage account right after passing, and the TCP growth plan scales Live accounts up to $400K
  • No minimum trading days, news trading allowed, no consistency rule or buffer on funded accounts
  • Weekly withdrawals from $100; activation fee ($139) is taken from the first withdrawal, not paid upfront

What to watch

  • Funded split is only 50% on any withdrawal under $2,250 (50K); 80% needs a larger single withdrawal
  • Evaluations are monthly subscriptions ($190 TCP50, $170 GAU50 list) that renew until cancelled
  • Live accounts use an intraday trailing drawdown and carry monthly data fees ($140/exchange on Rithmic)
  • No overnight holding in evaluations (flat by 3:50 PM CT); trade copiers banned; ladder breaches are a hard fail
  • Firm-published 2025 pass rate was 8.89%, and only about 18% of funded accounts had a withdrawal
