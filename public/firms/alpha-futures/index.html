End-of-day trailing drawdown

Your loss limit moves up only when a trading day closes at a new high balance. Open profits during the day do not drag it up, so a winning trade you give back intraday does not tighten the limit until the close.

Every Alpha Futures rule explained

What works

  • 90% profit split on all evaluation-based accounts
  • EOD trailing drawdown that stops at the starting balance
  • No activation fee on accounts bought after 2026-07-08
  • Advanced Qualified: no daily loss guard, no consistency rule, up to $15,000 per payout request
  • UK-registered company (Companies House 15655643) with published registered office

What to watch

  • Evaluations are monthly subscriptions that rebill until passed or cancelled
  • Bots/full automation prohibited; positions must be closed by 4:20 PM ET daily
  • Payouts need 5 winning days of $200+ and are limited to 50% of profit per request
  • Path-to-live Alpha Prime option has only a 60% split
  • Rules and promotions changed many times in 2026; 61 restricted countries incl. most of the Balkans
