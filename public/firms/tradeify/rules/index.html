End-of-day trailing drawdown

Your loss limit moves up only when a trading day closes at a new high balance. Open profits during the day do not drag it up, so a winning trade you give back intraday does not tighten the limit until the close.

Account sizes offered: 25K, 50K, 100K, 150K.

Drawdown

All plans use a $2,000 end-of-day trailing drawdown on 50K, enforced in real time. On funded accounts it locks at $50,100.

Daily loss limit

Growth and Lightning have a $1,250 soft daily limit that stops trading for the day without failing the account. The Select evaluation has none.

Consistency

Select evaluation: no day above 40% of total profit. Select funded: none. Growth evaluation: none. Growth funded: 35% on every payout request. Lightning: 20% for the first payout, 25% for the second, 30% after that.

Payouts

Select Flex pays after five winning days of $150+, up to 50% of profit and $2,500 per payout. Growth needs a $53,000 balance, a $500 minimum and five days of $150+, with caps rising from $1,500 to $3,000.

Not allowed

  • Holding positions past 4:45 PM ET or over the weekend.
  • High-frequency bots; any bot must be your own.
