Account sizes offered: 2K, 5K, 10K, 20K, 50K, 100K.

Drawdown

Standard 2 Step: static 8% ($4,000 on 50K). Standard 1 Step: trails the highest running equity, including open profit, by 5% ($2,500), with no lock level stated. Standard 3 Step: static 3% ($1,500).

Daily loss limit

4% on 2 Step, 3% on 1 Step and 2% on 3 Step, measured from the higher of balance or equity at 00:00 UTC.

Consistency and trading days

No consistency rule on any Standard plan. On 2 Step you need 3 profitable days of at least 0.5% in each phase and in each funded payout cycle. 1 Step and 3 Step have no minimum days.

Payouts

Every 10 business days, the first 10 business days after your first funded trade, with a $20 minimum. The split is 80%, or 90% with an add-on. The challenge fee is refunded after the third reward, and payouts need KYC. Since November 5, 2026, at most $10,000 of profit (before the split) can be withdrawn per trader across two payouts or any rolling 30-day period, all accounts combined; profit above that is removed.

News and holding

No opening or closing trades on affected instruments from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after red-folder releases; in the challenge this is a hard breach. Overnight and weekend holding are allowed.

Not allowed

  • EAs and automated trading. All trades must be placed manually.
  • Copying another person's trades.
