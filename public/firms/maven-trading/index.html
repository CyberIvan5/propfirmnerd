Maven Trading review

Checked October 19, 2026 against 10 official and public pages. How we check

Who Maven fits

Manual forex traders on a budget. The 1 Step uses an intraday trailing drawdown on equity, so open profit you give back counts against you. Note that Maven's terms let it close the accounts of traders who defame it on social media or review sites.

What works

  • Low entry prices: 50K Standard 2 Step $220 list ($198 with the published ETERNAL code); sizes from $2K
  • Static 8% max loss on the 2 Step and no consistency rule on Standard plans
  • Overnight and weekend holding allowed on all plans
  • Several payout routes (USDT, USDC, Rise Pay) with a $20 minimum
  • Operating since 2022; 1-, 2- and 3-step formats

What to watch

  • Trustpilot rating is withheld for a guidelines breach, with fake reviews removed (seen 2026-10-19)
  • T&Cs require traders not to 'defame' Maven on social media and review sites, with account termination as the stated consequence
  • EAs/bots are prohibited; news trading is restricted ±2 minutes on Standard plans
  • Fee is refunded only after the third reward; 1 Step uses an equity-based trailing max loss with no stated lock
  • Funded accounts merge to at most $200,000 combined; no scaling plan or live-capital route published
