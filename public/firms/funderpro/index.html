Who FunderPro fits

Forex traders who want a static max loss and accept an 80% split, or 90% with a paid add-on. One Phase adds a 40% best-day rule in the challenge, and Pro adds a 45% rule in both phases. Trustpilot currently withholds FunderPro's rating, so read recent payout reports before you buy.

What works

  • Challenge fee credited back with the first reward on One Phase and Classic
  • Static max loss (10% Classic/Pro, 6% One Phase) rather than trailing
  • Classic has no consistency rule; Pro Weekly pays a 90% split without an add-on
  • Pro Daily rewards can be requested any time the account is 1%+ in profit
  • News trading allowed in all challenge phases; EAs allowed if you own them

What to watch

  • Trustpilot rating is withheld for a guidelines breach and the profile carries a warning (seen 2026-10-19)
  • Default split is 80% on One Phase and Classic; 90% costs a paid add-on (+20% of the fee)
  • Weekend holding requires the paid Swing add-on; holding without it fails the account
  • Pro fee is not credited back; Pro Daily split is only 60%
  • Simulated accounts only; the T&Cs cap aggregate funded allocation at $200,000 even though the scaling plan advertises up to $5M
