Every FundedNext rule explained

What works

  • Large, established operation: Trustpilot 4.5 from about 81,760 reviews; FundedNext launched March 2022
  • Accepts US traders for both CFD and futures
  • Stellar 2-Step uses a static 10% max loss, and the fee is refunded with the first reward
  • Futures Rapid Pro has no daily loss limit, a 90% split, payouts every 3 days and no buffer
  • Many payout methods (crypto, Rise, bank, card)

What to watch

  • Default CFD split is 80%; higher splits need Scale-Up, add-ons or a different payout schedule
  • EAs banned on CFD accounts of $50K and above, and for all US CFD clients
  • Funded news-trading profits count at only 40% (±5 min window); 3% max-risk rule on funded
  • Rules changed several times in 2026 (Jan 12 split/refund changes, weekend-holding pause and reinstatement); Trustpilot summary notes complaints about accounts deactivated after moving to live
