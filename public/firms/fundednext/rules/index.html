Stellar 2-Step (50K)

Targets of 8% ($4,000) and 5% ($2,500), a 5% daily loss and a static 10% max loss, at least five trading days. First reward after 21 days, then every 14 days by default.

Stellar 1-Step (50K)

A 10% target, 3% daily loss and 6% static max loss. For accounts bought from January 12, 2026 the split is 80% and the fee comes back with the third reward.

Funded CFD rules

No more than 3% open risk at any time. Profit from trades within five minutes of high-impact news counts at only 40%. Trades closed within 30 seconds must stay below 30% of profit.

Futures Rapid Pro (50K)

$3,000 target and $2,000 end-of-day trailing drawdown that locks at $50,100. No consistency rule in the challenge, 40% once funded; payouts every three days, $250 to $1,200 per cycle.

Other rules

News tradingCFD: allowed in challenge phases; on funded accounts, profits from trades within ±5 minutes of high-impact news on correlated pairs count at only 40% (losses count in full). Futures: Flex page says news trading is allowed
Bots and EAsCFD: restricted. Allowed on MT4/MT5 for accounts below $50K (with conditions and a usage fee); not allowed at $50K and above, not on cTrader/Match-Trader, and not for US clients; prop-passing EAs are banned. Futures: automated bots/AI allowed per the Futures Challenge Terms (subject to review)
Copy tradingCFD: allowed only between Challenge accounts of the same trader; prohibited between different people and on any FundedNext (funded) account. Futures: copying/mirroring trades across multiple accounts is prohibited per the terms
Overnight and weekendCFD: overnight allowed on all account types; weekend holding allowed on Challenge and FundedNext accounts (reinstated 2026-05-08 after a pause from 2026-01-17). Futures: holding beyond market close is not allowed
