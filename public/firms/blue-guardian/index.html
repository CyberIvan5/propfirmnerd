What works

  • 2 Step Standard uses a static 8% max loss
  • Payouts processed within 24 business hours per help center
  • Instant funding option with on-demand payouts
  • Both forex/CFD and futures under one brand; overnight/weekend holding and own EAs allowed
  • Published tiered affiliate commissions

What to watch

  • Trustpilot rating suspended for a guideline breach (fake reviews removed)
  • 1 Step and Instant use trailing drawdown
  • Guardian Shield: 1-2% floating loss closes trades and first breach halves the split
  • 3% processing fee on every payout; strict no-refund policy
  • News trading restricted on funded accounts
