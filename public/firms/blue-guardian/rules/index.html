Account sizes offered: 5K, 10K, 25K, 50K, 100K, 200K, 300K, 400K.

Drawdown

2 Step Standard has a static max loss of $4,000 on 50K. 1 Step Standard and Instant Standard use a trailing max loss of $3,000.

Daily loss limit

$2,000 on the 2 Step and 1 Step Standard; $1,500 on Instant Standard.

Consistency

Instant Standard has a 20% consistency rule.

Payouts

Challenge accounts pay every 14 days, with an 85% split (90% as a paid add-on). Instant Standard pays on demand after 5 profitable days, at 80%. Rise payouts have a $500 minimum; crypto payouts have a $100 minimum and must be under $5,000. Blue Guardian charges 3% on payouts.

News and holding

News trading is allowed in challenges. On funded and instant accounts you may not open or close trades within 5 minutes before or after high-impact (red folder) news and FOMC, and profits from those trades can be removed. Instant Standard accounts bought after November 13, 2025 may not trade news at all. Overnight and weekend holding are allowed on forex and CFD accounts.

Not allowed

  • HFT, tick scalping (trades under 2 minutes are flagged) and arbitrage bots.
  • Copying another trader, or hedging across accounts.
