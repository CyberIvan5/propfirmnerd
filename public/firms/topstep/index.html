End-of-day trailing drawdown

Your loss limit moves up only when a trading day closes at a new high balance. Open profits during the day do not drag it up, so a winning trade you give back intraday does not tighten the limit until the close.

Who Topstep fits

Day traders who close every position by 3:10 PM CT and want an established firm with a published route from simulated to live trading. You choose between the Standard path ($49/month, $149 activation) and the No Activation Fee path ($95/month). New traders trade on TopstepX.

What to know first

The Trading Combine has a consistency target: your best day must be at or below 55% of the profit target. Going over does not fail you; it raises the target. Topstep publishes no changelog, and several rules changed during 2026.

Every Topstep rule explained

What works

  • Low entry price: $49/month for the 50K Standard Combine.
  • Choice of Standard (lower monthly price, $149 activation) or No Activation Fee path ($95/month, $0 activation).
  • Optional daily loss limit; hitting it only pauses trading and is not a rule violation.
  • Many payout methods, including same-day Prop-to-Brokerage and Aeropay for US traders; $125 minimum payout.
  • Defined path to a live account with uncapped payouts and daily payouts after 30 winning days.

What to watch

  • Monthly subscription continues until you pass or cancel.
  • XFA per-request payout cap is $2,000 (Standard) or $3,000 (Consistency) on 50K, without the limited-time DLL offer.
  • TopstepX is the only platform listed for new traders; VPS/VPN use is prohibited.
  • Lower Trustpilot score than peers (3.7 from about 15,000 reviews).
  • Frequent 2026 rule and payout changes; Topstep's help articles show relative dates only, with no changelog.

Questions traders ask

How much is Topstep 50K?

$49 a month on the Standard path with a $149 activation fee when you pass, or $95 a month with no activation fee, as of October 16, 2026.

Does Topstep use trailing drawdown?

Yes, end-of-day trailing. The Maximum Loss Limit rises with your end-of-day balance and never moves down.

What drawdown does Topstep use?

Topstep's main 50K plan uses end-of-day trailing drawdown with a max loss of $2,000, as of October 16, 2026.
