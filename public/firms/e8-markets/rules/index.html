Account sizes offered: 5K, 10K, 25K, 50K, 100K, 150K, 200K, 400K, 500K.

Drawdown

E8 One trails the highest closed balance and locks at the starting balance; the default is 6% ($3,000 on 50K) and it can be configured at checkout. E8 Pro uses a static 8% max loss ($4,000) that never trails. E8 Signature uses end-of-day trailing drawdown ($2,000).

Daily loss limit

$2,000 on E8 One and $1,250 on E8 Pro.

Consistency

E8 One: none in the challenge, 40% in the performance stage. E8 Pro: no consistency rule, but a $1,000 daily profit cap. E8 Signature: none in the challenge, 35% in the performance stage.

Payouts

E8 One pays on demand, E8 Pro daily, and E8 Signature on demand after 5 profitable days. The split is 80% by default; E8 One can be set to 90% or 100% at checkout and E8 Pro to 100%.

News and holding

News trading is allowed on E8 Pro, E8 Signature and in the E8 One challenge. E8 One performance accounts may not open or close trades from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after high-impact news. E8 One and E8 Pro allow overnight and weekend holding; E8 Signature closes positions automatically at 23:00 server time.

Not allowed

  • Running the same trades or strategy as other users. Bots are allowed on E8 One, E8 Pro and Signature Classic and Perpetuals, but not on the futures products.
  • Cooperating with other traders, or hedging across accounts.
