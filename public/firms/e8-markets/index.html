E8 Markets review

Checked October 16, 2026 against 27 official and public pages. How we check

E8 Markets is based in Dallas and accepts US traders. Its 50K E8 One costs $288 with a $4,500 target, while E8 Pro ($218) offers a static drawdown and daily payouts with a $1,000 daily profit cap. Trustpilot currently shows no rating for E8 after removing reviews it says were fake.

Who E8 fits

Traders who want to tune their challenge: E8 One lets you change the drawdown, daily limit and split at checkout. Fees are non-refundable, and E8 changed its product lineup several times in 2025 and 2026.

What works

  • US-based entity (Dallas, TX) and US clients accepted, with a US-only Aeropay payout option
  • No activation fees; one-time fee on all products
  • E8 Pro: static drawdown, daily payouts, no payout caps
  • Highly customizable E8 One (drawdown, daily limit, split up to 100%)
  • E8 says it has paid out over $79M to traders

What to watch

  • Trustpilot rating suspended: 'rating is unavailable due to a breach of our guidelines' and Trustpilot says it removed fake reviews
  • Fees non-refundable 'under any circumstance' per Terms
  • E8 One uses a trailing (dynamic) drawdown and bans news trading in the Performance stage
  • Signature payouts capped and limited to 4-5 payouts per account; caps reduced for futures in Oct 2026
  • Frequent product changes (Dec 2025 rebuild, June 2026 Pro V2, Oct 2026 Signature changes)
