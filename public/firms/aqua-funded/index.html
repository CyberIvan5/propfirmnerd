Aqua Funded review

Checked October 19, 2026 against 33 official and public pages. How we check

Who Aqua Funded fits

Forex traders who want a cheap entry and a high split, and who can work with a trailing drawdown on the 1 Step and Instant models. News trading is allowed, but profit made within 5 minutes of red-folder news is capped at 0.5% of the starting balance per payout cycle.

What works

  • 90% split on all main models, 100% available as an add-on
  • News trading (with a 0.5% profit cap), weekend holding, EAs and own-account trade copiers allowed
  • Evaluation fee refunded with the fourth payout; 24-business-hour payout guarantee or an extra $1,000
  • Four platforms: MT5, cTrader, TradeLocker, Match-Trader
  • Low list prices at 50K ($239 to $305 for the main models) plus a 25% public code

What to watch

  • Trustpilot shows no score: rating unavailable for a guideline breach and fake reviews removed (1,210 reviews, 39% one-star)
  • 3% processing fee on every payout; first two rewards capped at $10,000 on 100K+ accounts
  • Trailing drawdown on 1 Step Standard (6%) and Instant Pro (6%), plus floating-loss-per-trade limits on instant models
  • Fee refund only at the fourth payout, although the checkout says '100% refundable'
  • Many models with rules that change by purchase date; US eligibility not stated
