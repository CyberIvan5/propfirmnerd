Questions traders ask

Why is the real cost higher than the listed price?

Monthly plans rebill until you pass, many traders reset or rebuy after a fail, and some firms charge an activation fee when you pass. A discount usually applies only to the first payment.

What does break-even profit mean here?

It is how much your funded account must earn before your share of the profit covers what you spent. It assumes the split you enter and ignores taxes and payout fees.
