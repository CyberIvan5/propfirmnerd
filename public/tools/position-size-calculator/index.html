How it works

Risk per contract is the stop in ticks times the contract's tick value. The number of contracts is your risk divided by that, rounded down. Slippage and commissions are not included, so leave a margin, especially on fast markets and news.

Prop firms also cap how many contracts you may hold, and some scale the cap with your profit. Check the cap on the firm's review; it can be lower than what this calculator allows.

Questions traders ask

How much should I risk per trade in a prop firm evaluation?

There is no rule that fits everyone, but the math is simple: if each loss costs 10% of your max loss, ten losses in a row end the account. Many traders size so a full stop costs a small share of the max loss and stay well inside the daily loss limit.

Why does the calculator round down?

You cannot trade part of a contract, and rounding up would risk more than you entered.
