MNQ tick value

One MNQ tick (0.25 index points) is worth $0.50, and one full point is worth $2. MNQ is one tenth the size of NQ, which is why prop firms often let you trade 10 micros for every mini.

What a typical MNQ trade risks

A 20-point stop on one MNQ contract is 80 ticks, or $40. On five contracts it is $200. On a 50K evaluation with a $2,000 max loss, that is 10% of your entire drawdown on one trade. Use the position size calculator to size from your stop instead of picking a contract count first.

Questions traders ask

How much is 1 point on MNQ?

One point on MNQ is worth $2 per contract. A 10-point move is $20 per contract.

How many MNQ equal one NQ?

Ten. NQ is $20 per point and MNQ is $2 per point.

What is the MNQ tick size?

0.25 index points, worth $0.50.
