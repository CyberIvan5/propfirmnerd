Prop firm rule changes: November 2026

Updated November 23, 2026. Each change links to the firm's own announcement or rules page.

Prop firms change prices, drawdown rules and payout terms often, and usually without much notice. This page lists every change we recorded while checking the firms we track: 14 changes at 12 firms in November 2026 so far, with earlier months below.

November 2026

  • Alpha Futures: Zero 50K evaluation price is now $169/month (was $139) Source
  • Aqua Funded: 2 Step Standard 50K list price raised to $279 (from $239), per Aqua Funded's own pricing widget (seen 2026-11-10) Source
  • Blue Guardian: Futures Express can no longer be purchased from 16 Nov 2026; existing Express accounts keep their rules Source
  • BrightFunded: BrightFunded Futures is live: BrightPlus and BrightCore plans from $25K to $150K, up to 5 funded accounts and $750,000 per household (seen 2026-11-06; launch date not stated) Source
  • BrightFunded: CFD relaunch for accounts bought from 16 Nov 2026: 1-Step renamed 1-Step Daily with daily payouts and no minimum days; 2-Step Bright needs 3 profitable days per phase (was 5 trading days) and the first payout comes after 14 days (was 30); new Instant plan Source
  • BrightFunded: 2-Step Classic (10%/5%, 5% daily, 10% static, €297 at 50K) retired: 15 Nov 2026 was the last day to buy it; existing accounts are not affected Source
  • Earn2Trade: Progression Ladder retired for subscriptions bought from 2 Nov 2026: fixed 4 contracts / 40 micros on 50K; funded traders now need 5 profitable days of $150+ (50K) before each withdrawal Source
  • FundedNext: Futures Rapid Pro 50K payout cap now $1,000 per withdrawal for withdrawals 1-3 and $1,250 for withdrawals 4-5 (was $1,200 per cycle; seen 2026-11-16, change date not stated) Source
  • FundingPips: 2 Step Standard reward cycles are now Bi-weekly (80%) or On Demand (100%); the 60% weekly option is no longer listed Source
  • Goat Funded Trader: 50K list prices at checkout: 2 Step GOAT $330 (was $308), 1 Step GOAT $247 (was $314); the homepage now features a limited-time '2 Step Promo' 100K account for $99 with its own rules Source
  • Lucid Trading: LucidFlex 50K list price lowered to $115 (from $136) and its reset to $80 (from $95), per Lucid's own store (seen 2026-11-10) Source
  • Maven Trading: Terms v2026.3: at most $10,000 of profit (before the split) can be withdrawn per trader across two payouts or any rolling 30-day period, combined across Standard, BNPL and Instant accounts; profit above the cap is removed Source
  • Phidias: Lower list prices: 50K Fundamental $90 a month (was $164) or $225 one-time; 50K Premium $110 a month (was $329) or $275 one-time Source
  • TradeDay: Evaluations switched from monthly subscriptions to one-time fees ('No subscriptions'); 50K list prices now Quick Pay Intraday $124 (was $131/month), Quick Pay EOD $174 (was $175/month), Fast Pass $248 (was $189/month); promo code now SALE60 (60% off) Source

October 2026

  • Alpha Capital Group: My Alpha trading journal launched (homepage banner code MYALPHA, 30% off) Source
  • Blueberry Funded: Synthetic Challenge replaced by Synthetic 1-Step, 2-Step and Instant Funding; 24 new synthetic instruments; EAs allowed but grid/martingale banned; 25% funded consistency rule and 5-minute minimum hold Source
  • E8 Markets: E8 Signature Futures bought after 1 Oct 2026 17:00 UTC: max payouts cut from 5 to 4, 3rd/4th payout caps lowered (50K: $2,250 to $2,000), 150K EOD drawdown $4,500 to $4,000 Source
  • E8 Markets: E8 Pro 50K list price shown as $228 (was $218); 8% profit target ($4,000) and 8% static drawdown ($4,000) Source
  • FTMO: 1-Step: Consistency rule (best day at most 50% of total profit) replaces the Best Day Rule for new orders and merged accounts Source
  • FXIFY: New general Terms and Conditions document published (2026 version, uploaded Oct 2026; contents not read) Source
  • Funding Traders: Help center adds '2-Step Day & Swing' variants (no exact date; article shows 'Updated this week') Source
  • Funding Traders: New Instant Funding rules for accounts bought from this date: 100% split, on-demand payouts, 15% consistency, 6% max loss that trails until +3% then locks, weekend holding allowed Source
  • FundingPips: Reward cycles listed as Weekly (60%), Bi-Weekly (80%) and On Demand (100%, 35% consistency); no monthly option shown Source
  • Goat Funded Trader: VPNs, VPSs, proxies and hosting services banned during trading on any account from 6 Oct 2026 Source
  • Goat Funded Trader: Copy trading between own GFT accounts allowed; GOAT Trade Copier launched; min hold time cut from 2 to 1 minute (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • Lucid Trading: LucidFlex evaluation consistency is 40% as standard; 50% is now a paid add-on Source

September 2026

  • Aqua Funded: Instant Funding Pro consistency rule eased from 15% to 20% for new purchases (described as limited-time) Source
  • Blue Guardian: Futures Express: from 2nd payout, new profit equal to 50% of previous payout required (accounts bought from 2 Sep) Source
  • Funding Traders: New Terms of Service version effective (FT US Inc and Funding Traders Group Ltd., Saint Lucia) Source
  • Hola Prime: Forex copy trading on funded accounts limited to one master and one copier account, for accounts bought on or after 15 Sep 2026 Source
  • Tradeify: Select 50K payout caps changed for new accounts: Flex cap $3,000 -> $2,500; Daily cap $1,000 -> $1,250. Source

August 2026

  • Alpha Futures: Path to Live structure updated (Alpha Prime 60% + salary vs Live 80%) Source
  • Aqua Funded: 1 Step Flex max loss raised from 10% to 12% static; 2 Step Pro funded consistency rule changed from 25% to 50% (new purchases) Source
  • Blue Guardian: 1 Step Standard target cut from 10% to 9%; 2 Step Standard min trading days cut from 5 to 3 (accounts bought from 20 Aug) Source
  • Blueberry Funded: Flex 1-Step launched (12% target, 12% static max loss, 85% split); 2-Step Challenge retired for new purchases; Prime minimum active days cut to 3; Instant Lite relaunched with no minimum days and a 15% consistency check Source
  • Blueberry Funded: Help center notice: some rewards taking up to 7-14 business days while payment infrastructure is upgraded (normal 1-2 business days) Source
  • BrightFunded: Free $1K Challenge (F1KC) offered: free 1-phase evaluation with 1-Step rules, 80% split, no 15% profit reward (help article dated 2026-08-26; launch date not stated) Source
  • Bulenox: Commission rate sheet updated Source
  • Bulenox: Fast Track and Momentum launched; Qualification switched from monthly subscription to one-time 30-day access; 250K size removed; max active Master accounts cut from 11 to 5 (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • Goat Funded Trader: 1-Step daily drawdown cut from 4% to 3% for accounts bought from 1 Aug 2026 Source
  • Lucid Trading: Daily loss limit made optional (On/Off at purchase) on LucidFlex, LucidPro and LucidDaily; DLL Off costs more. Source
  • MyFundedFutures: Cutoff date for Legacy Subscriptions in the Terms Source
  • MyFundedFutures: New evaluation purchases switched to one-time payments with no renewal cycle Source
  • The5ers: Summer Plan ($100K from $149) extended through September, then until further notice (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • Topstep: Wise payouts restored for traders in China, Canada and the UK. Source
  • TradeDay: Rithmic platforms added Source

July 2026

  • Alpha Capital Group: Max Risk Rule for Qualified accounts bought after this date: max risk per asset 3% ($5K-$25K), 2% ($50K-$200K), 1% (Alpha Direct); breach closes the account Source
  • Alpha Futures: Payout frequency changed to up to 4 times per month; Zero daily loss limit replaced by a 2% soft Daily Loss Guard; 90% flat split (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • Alpha Futures: Direct (no-evaluation) account launched Source
  • Alpha Futures: $149 Advanced activation fee removed for accounts purchased after this date Source
  • Alpha Futures: New Terms and Conditions effective Source
  • Earn2Trade: Help center article dated 6 Jul 2026 states that exceeding the TCP progression ladder contract limit is a hard fail that ends the evaluation Source
  • Funded Futures Network: STEADY account (one-time fee) introduced, replacing Express MAX for new purchases; sim-funded profits forfeited on move to live for accounts bought after this date Source
  • Funded Futures Network: Max 5 active funded accounts; live split 90/10; Standard MAX sim split 80/20, STEADY 90/10 Source
  • Goat Funded Trader: Funded profitable-day requirement raised from 3 to 4 per payout (2-Step accounts from 25 Jul, 1-Step from 27 Jul) Source
  • Goat Funded Trader: Resets offered at every stage; new Instant HERO and Instant Premium plans (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • Maven Trading: 2 Step Omo challenge described on Maven's blog (6% / 8% targets, 4% daily, 8% static, 4 profitable days, M2 Account Saver); launch date not stated Source
  • MyFundedFutures: Builder plan and Rapid EOD plan (50K, then 25K) launched (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • TradeDay: Fast Pass Funded Sim: 50K payout cap cut from $2,000 to $1,500 and 45% funded consistency rule added for accounts opened on or after this date Source

June 2026

  • E8 Markets: E8 Pro V2 launched: 8% target, 8% static drawdown, no consistency rule, daily payouts (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • Topstep: Topstep Labs launched limited 'drops', starting with a $25K Static Drawdown Trading Combine (June) and a $50K Static Drawdown Combine (October 2026). Source
  • Topstep: Limited-time offer: adding a Daily Loss Limit at checkout on a new Trading Combine doubles XFA per-request payout caps. Source

May 2026

  • Alpha Capital Group: Alpha Three no longer offered for new purchases (stated in the firm's rules post dated 2026-05-20) Source
  • Funded Futures Network: HFT policy: max 30% of trades of 5 seconds or less per session Source
  • FundedNext: Weekend holding reinstated for Stellar 1-Step, 2-Step and Lite accounts (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • Funding Traders: Evaluation fee refund for 1-Step and 2-Step moved from the first payout to the second payout for accounts bought on or after this date Source
  • Hola Prime: Announced a 'Zero Payout Denials Policy' applying to all accounts Source
  • TradeDay: TradeDay 2.0: Quick Pay replaces the old evaluation (day-one payouts, milestones removed); new Fast Pass program; defined route to Funded Live (up to 5 Live accounts, 90% split); old programs no longer sold Source

April 2026

  • Aqua Funded: News trading allowed on accounts from this date, with news-window profit capped at 0.5% of starting balance per payout cycle (previously banned on funded accounts) Source
  • Blueberry Funded: All MT4 and DXtrade accounts migrated to MT5 Source
  • BrightFunded: 'BrightFunded 2.0': new 2-Step Bright (8%/5%, 4% daily, 8% static), 2-Step Classic (10%/5%, 5% daily, 10% static) and 1-Step (10%, 3% daily, 6% trailing) plans. Accounts bought before keep the Original 2-Step rules (8%/5%, 5% daily, 10% static) Source
  • Bulenox: Profit split changed to 100% of first $10,000 then 90%; payout frequency changed to weekly on Wednesdays; drawdown changed from balance-based to trailing with EOD option (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • FXIFY: 2 Phase Pro launched with static drawdown (30 April 2026) Source
  • Funded Futures Network: Per-account payout caps introduced for OG and MAX sim-funded accounts (50K: $1,500 for payouts 1-3, $2,000 after); older 7PV OG accounts exempt Source
  • Topstep: Third-party trackers report loss-limit documentation briefly switched to static daily limits (about April 21) and back to EOD trailing (about May 10); not confirmed on Topstep's own pages. Source
  • Tradeify: Tradeify 3.0: all plans one-time purchase only (no subscriptions), Select/Growth prices up about 3%, Select 50K target back to $3,000 for new accounts, Rithmic and Tradesea added. Source

March 2026

  • Apex Trader Funding: New EOD 50K accounts require 5 days of at least $250 net profit before a payout request. Source
  • Apex Trader Funding: Previous evaluation and PA products became 'Legacy' and closed to new purchases; new EOD and Intraday evaluation products launched as one-time purchases with 30-day access, no reset fees, 50% PA consistency, 6-payout cap per PA, and tier-based PA scaling/DLL. Source
  • Blueberry Funded: Rule overhaul for new accounts: 1.5% risk-per-trade-idea hard limit on funded accounts; martingale/grid, position stacking, all-in trading, lot size restriction and others removed from the prohibited list; Rapid Evaluation discontinued Source
  • FundedNext: US traders can no longer buy new cTrader accounts; US CFD clients use Match-Trader only Source
  • The5ers: New High Stakes entry option added: 10% Step 1 / 5% Step 2 at a lower price; Classic 8%/5% kept (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • Tradeify: New Select plan (3-day evaluation, Flex or Daily payout choice); live program rebranded Tradeify Elite with one live account per funded account that received a payout, no simulated-profit cap. Source

February 2026

  • FTMO: 1-Step FTMO Challenge launched (10% target, 3% daily, 10% EOD trailing max loss, 90% split, 50% Best Day rule) (reported by a third-party tracker) Source

January 2026

  • BrightFunded: 15% Evaluation Profit Reward introduced: 15% of evaluation profit added to a new Funded Account after 10% funded growth and a payout (paid challenges only) Source
  • FundedNext: Stellar product update: 1-Step default split cut from 90% to 80% (90% after Scale-Up); 1-Step fee refund moved from the 1st to the 3rd reward; 15% challenge reward tied to Scale-Up Source
  • FundedNext: Weekend holding paused on Stellar funded accounts (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • FunderPro: Trustpilot placed a warning on FunderPro's profile and the rating is shown as unavailable due to a guidelines breach; FunderPro published a response on 2026-01-19 Source
  • Hola Prime: Futures 1-Step Prime: max loss set at 4% EOD trailing for $25K and $50K accounts bought on or after 19 Jan 2026 (3% on $100K/$150K) Source
  • MyFundedFutures: Rapid sim-funded profit split set to 90% Source
  • Topstep: New traders get a flat 90/10 split; those who joined the new dashboard before this date keep 100% of their first $10,000. Source

Earlier in 2026

  • Apex Trader Funding: Legacy accounts relaunched as a limited-time, monthly-billed offer (page last modified 2026-08-25). Source
  • E8 Markets: Lineup cut to E8 One and a rebuilt 1-step E8 Signature; older programs removed (reported by a third-party tracker) Source
  • FundedNext: Futures lineup now Rapid Pro, Rapid Daily, Legacy and Flex (plus Direct); Flex sold with 'launch price' Source
  • Lucid Trading: New live structure: one live account per funded account with a payout, starting at $0 with daily payouts, plus a cash-out option. Source
  • MyFundedFutures: Pro plan redesigned (live Reserve program, $100K sim payout cap per user); old Pro accounts keep old rules. Exact date not stated. Source
  • Phidias: Discretionary path-to-LIVE threshold raised to 5 payouts or $100,000 cumulative profit; previous 3 payouts / $75,000 marked deprecated (About page still shows old thresholds) Source
