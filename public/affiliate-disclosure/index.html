Affiliate disclosure

Updated October 16, 2026. PropFirmNerd editorial team

Some links on PropFirmNerd are affiliate links. When you click one and buy an evaluation, the prop firm may pay us a commission. You pay the same price, and when a firm gives us a discount code, you usually pay less.

Links to firms go through addresses that start with propfirmnerd.com/go/ so we can change a link in one place. Some of them are affiliate links and some are plain links to the firm's site.

Commissions do not change the rules, prices or dates we publish, and they do not decide the order of our lists. We review and list firms whether or not we have a deal with them, and we do not accept payment for reviews, rankings, or removing information.

Questions? Contact us.
