Which to pick

Pick Blueberry Prime if you want no risk-per-trade limit and no lot restriction, with payouts every 14 days after 3 active days. Its Flex 1-Step gives a 12% static max loss ($6,000) for $490, but bans weekend holding and caps risk at 1% per trade idea. Pick FTMO for the lower price on a 10% static account, the fee refund and the longer track record.

Rules that trip people up

Blueberry bans opening or closing trades from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after high-impact news on every account; on Prime only new positions are blocked. FTMO applies its 2-minute news rule on funded accounts only.

Is Blueberry Funded or FTMO cheaper?

Blueberry Funded's main 50K plan lists at $302.50 and FTMO's at €345, before discounts, as checked on November 9, 2026 and October 16, 2026. The prices are in different currencies, so convert before you compare. Use the true cost calculator for the full cost.

Does Blueberry Funded or FTMO have a consistency rule?

Blueberry Funded has no consistency rule on its main plan. FTMO has no consistency rule on its main plan.
