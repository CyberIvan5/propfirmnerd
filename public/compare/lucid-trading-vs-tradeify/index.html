Lucid Trading vs Tradeify

Checked October 26, 2026 against both firms' own pages. Some links earn us a commission. Disclosure

Lucid ($115 for LucidFlex) and Tradeify ($145 for Growth) are two of the cheapest one-time 50K evaluations we track, both with end-of-day drawdown and no activation fee. Lucid allows bots and has no consistency rule once funded on LucidFlex; Tradeify allows only your own non-HFT bots and adds a 35% rule to Growth payouts.

Which to pick

Pick Lucid if you trade with your own automation or copiers. Pick Tradeify if you want a choice of payout styles (Select Flex or Select Daily). Lucid restricts more countries, so check that it serves yours.

Questions traders ask

Is Lucid Trading or Tradeify cheaper?

Lucid Trading's main 50K plan lists at $115 and Tradeify's at $145, before discounts, as checked on October 16, 2026 and October 26, 2026. Lucid Trading has the lower list price. Use the true cost calculator for the full cost.

What is the difference between Lucid Trading and Tradeify drawdown?

Lucid Trading uses end-of-day trailing drawdown with a 50K max loss of $2,000 on its main plan. Tradeify uses end-of-day trailing drawdown with a 50K max loss of $2,000 on its main plan. Figures as checked on October 16, 2026 and October 26, 2026.

Does Lucid Trading or Tradeify have a consistency rule?

Lucid Trading applies a consistency rule in the evaluation only. Tradeify applies a consistency rule on funded accounts only.

Do Lucid Trading and Tradeify accept US traders?

Yes. Both Lucid Trading and Tradeify accept US residents, according to their own pages (as checked on October 16, 2026 and October 26, 2026).
