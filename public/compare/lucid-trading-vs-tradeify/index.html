Lucid Trading vs Tradeify

Checked October 26, 2026 against both firms' own pages.

Lucid ($136 for LucidFlex) and Tradeify ($145 for Growth) are two of the cheapest one-time 50K evaluations we track, both with end-of-day drawdown and no activation fee. Lucid allows bots and has no consistency rule once funded on LucidFlex; Tradeify allows only your own non-HFT bots and adds a 35% rule to Growth payouts.

Which to pick

Pick Lucid if you trade with your own automation or copiers. Pick Tradeify if you want a choice of payout styles (Select Flex or Select Daily). Lucid restricts more countries, so check that it serves yours.
