Topstep vs MyFundedFutures

Checked October 16, 2026 against both firms' own pages.

MyFundedFutures sells one-time evaluations (from $153 for a 50K Builder) with no activation fee. Topstep charges $49 a month plus a $149 activation fee on its Standard path. Both use a $2,000 end-of-day drawdown on 50K evaluations. MyFundedFutures pays more often (every 48 hours on Builder, daily on Rapid), but Builder pays 80% against Topstep's 90%.

Which to pick

Pick MyFundedFutures if you want frequent payouts and a single payment. Its Rapid plan pays 90%, but its sim funded account uses an intraday trailing drawdown. Rapid EOD keeps it at end of day for a higher price. Pick Topstep if you prefer a monthly price and an end-of-day drawdown in the funded account.

News trading

MyFundedFutures requires you to be flat from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after Tier 1 releases on Rapid and Pro funded accounts. It allows news trading in every evaluation and on Builder. Topstep only bans purposely trading your full maximum size into a scheduled major news event.
