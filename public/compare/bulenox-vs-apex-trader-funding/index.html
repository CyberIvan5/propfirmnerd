Bulenox vs Apex Trader Funding

Checked October 16, 2026 against both firms' own pages.

Bulenox gives its 50K end-of-day account a wider $2,500 drawdown, has no consistency rule in the Qualification and allows news trading with no blackout window, for $175 one-time. Apex keeps the 50K drawdown at $2,000 and pays 100% of approved payouts on its performance accounts. It also adds a 50% consistency rule once you are funded and closes each performance account after six payouts. Apex's current evaluation prices were not shown on its site when we checked.

Which to pick

Pick Bulenox if you want more room on a 50K account and trade around news releases. Pick Apex if you want to keep all of each approved payout and do not mind its funded consistency rule and six-payout limit per account. Neither firm lets you hold positions overnight. Apex does not allow automated trading at all. Bulenox needs management approval for third-party algorithms.

Getting to a live account

Both decide at their discretion. Bulenox may consider traders for a live account after three successful payouts. Apex says it may move a trader to live at any time, and lists three consecutive withdrawals from one account as one guideline.
