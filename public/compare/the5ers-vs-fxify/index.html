The5ers vs FXIFY

Checked October 16, 2026 against both firms' own pages. Some links earn us a commission. Disclosure

The5ers' High Stakes costs $249 (New) or $279 (Classic) for 50K, with a static 8% max loss and payouts capped at $3,000 per request. FXIFY's Two Phase costs $379 with a static 10% max loss, and its One Phase trails the closed balance by 6%. The5ers needs written approval for any automated trading; FXIFY's homepage says EAs, including grid and martingale, are allowed.

Which to pick

Pick The5ers for the lower price, a scaling plan that lifts the split to 100%, and a record going back to 2016 with a 4.7 Trustpilot score. Pick FXIFY if you trade with EAs or want the larger 10% static loss limit.

Rules that trip people up

The5ers charges 3.5% on Rise, crypto and bank payouts (Hub Credits are free) and blocks new orders from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after high-impact news. FXIFY does not accept US residents.

Is The5ers or FXIFY cheaper?

The5ers' main 50K plan lists at $249 and FXIFY's at $379, before discounts, as of October 16, 2026. The5ers has the lower list price. Use the true cost calculator for the full cost.
