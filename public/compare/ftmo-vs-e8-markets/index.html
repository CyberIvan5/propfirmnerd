Which to pick

Pick FTMO for a loss limit that never moves, no consistency rule and a long track record. Its 1-Step (€319) trails end-of-day by 10% and has a 50% best-day rule. Pick E8 if you want to set drawdown, split (up to 100%) or payout speed yourself. E8 Pro ($228) has a static 8% max loss and daily payouts, but caps daily profit at $1,000.

Rules that trip people up

E8 One adds a 40% consistency rule in the performance stage. On news, FTMO funded accounts may not execute orders 2 minutes before or after selected high-impact releases, while E8 One performance accounts are blocked from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after.

Questions traders ask

Is FTMO or E8 Markets cheaper?

FTMO's main 50K plan lists at €345 and E8 Markets' at $288, before discounts, as of October 16, 2026. The prices are in different currencies, so convert before you compare. Use the true cost calculator for the full cost.

What is the difference between FTMO and E8 Markets drawdown?

FTMO uses static drawdown with a 50K max loss of €5,000 on its main plan. E8 Markets uses trailing on closed balance drawdown with a 50K max loss of $3,000 on its main plan. Figures as of October 16, 2026.

Does FTMO or E8 Markets have a consistency rule?

FTMO has no consistency rule on its main plan. E8 Markets applies a consistency rule on funded accounts only.

Do FTMO and E8 Markets accept US traders?

Yes. Both FTMO and E8 Markets accept US residents, according to their own pages (as of October 16, 2026).
