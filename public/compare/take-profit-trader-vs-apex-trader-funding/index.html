Take Profit Trader vs Apex Trader Funding

Checked October 16, 2026 against both firms' own pages.

Take Profit Trader charges $170 a month for a 50K Test and lets funded PRO traders withdraw from the first day above the buffer. Apex sells one-time evaluations with no consistency rule, and pays 100% of approved payouts on its performance accounts. It limits each performance account to six payouts. Neither firm allows bots.

Which to pick

Pick Take Profit Trader if you want withdrawals available early. Its PRO account pays 80%, or 90% on PRO+, and needs the balance above $52,000 on 50K before the first withdrawal. Pick Apex if you would rather pay once and keep 100% of approved payouts, and you can live with a funded consistency rule: no single day may be 50% or more of profit since the last payout.

Drawdown

Both use a $2,000 maximum loss on 50K. Take Profit Trader trails at end of day in the Test and intraday in PRO. Apex lets you choose an end-of-day or an intraday trailing evaluation.
