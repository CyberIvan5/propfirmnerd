FXIFY vs FTMO

Which to pick

Pick FTMO for its track record (since 2015), its 4.8 Trustpilot score against FXIFY's 4.3, or if you live in the US. Pick FXIFY if you run EAs, or want a one-phase account whose 6% drawdown trails the closed balance and locks at the starting balance. FXIFY's Two Phase has a static 10% max loss, like FTMO's 2-Step.

How much you can hold

FTMO caps you at $400,000 per trader or strategy before scaling. FXIFY lets you hold one active account per account size, up to $400K.

Questions traders ask

Is FXIFY or FTMO cheaper?

FXIFY's main 50K plan lists at $379 and FTMO's at €345, before discounts, as of October 16, 2026. The prices are in different currencies, so convert before you compare. Use the true cost calculator for the full cost.

What is the difference between FXIFY and FTMO drawdown?

FXIFY uses trailing on closed balance drawdown with a 50K max loss of $3,000 on its main plan. FTMO uses static drawdown with a 50K max loss of €5,000 on its main plan. Figures as of October 16, 2026.

Do FXIFY and FTMO accept US traders?

FTMO accepts US residents and FXIFY does not, according to their own pages (as of October 16, 2026).
