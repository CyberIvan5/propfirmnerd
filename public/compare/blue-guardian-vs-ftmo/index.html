Blue Guardian vs FTMO

Checked October 16, 2026 against both firms' own pages. Some links earn us a commission. Disclosure

Blue Guardian's 2 Step Standard costs $232 for 50K against €345 for FTMO's 2-Step. Both use a static max loss: $4,000 (8%) at Blue Guardian and 10% at FTMO. Blue Guardian pays 85% every 14 days and charges 3% on payouts; FTMO pays 80% (90% with scaling) from day 14 and says it charges no withdrawal commission.

Which to pick

Pick Blue Guardian if the entry price matters most. Pick FTMO for the larger loss limit, the fee refunded with your first reward and a 4.8 Trustpilot score.

Rules that trip people up

On funded accounts Blue Guardian blocks opening or closing trades within 5 minutes of high-impact news and FOMC; FTMO's window is 2 minutes around selected releases. Blue Guardian flags trades shorter than 2 minutes as tick scalping and bans HFT and arbitrage bots.
