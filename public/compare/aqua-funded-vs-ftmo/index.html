Aqua Funded vs FTMO

Checked October 19, 2026 against both firms' own pages. Some links earn us a commission. Disclosure

Aqua Funded's 2 Step Standard costs $279 for 50K with a static 8% max loss ($4,000) and a 90% split, but takes a 3% processing fee from every payout. FTMO's 2-Step costs €345 with a static 10% max loss, pays 80% (90% with scaling) and refunds the fee with the first reward. Aqua had no Trustpilot rating when we checked; FTMO is rated 4.8.

Which to pick

Pick Aqua Funded for the lower price, the higher split, instant-funding options and bots allowed for your own strategy. Pick FTMO for the larger loss limit, no withdrawal commission and the longer track record.

Rules that trip people up

On Aqua accounts from April 27, 2026, profit from trades within 5 minutes of red-folder news or FOMC is capped at 0.5% of the starting balance per payout cycle. FTMO funded accounts may not execute orders 2 minutes before or after selected high-impact news. Aqua allows weekend holding on every account; FTMO only in the challenge and on Swing accounts.

Is Aqua Funded or FTMO cheaper?

Aqua Funded's main 50K plan lists at $279 and FTMO's at €345, before discounts, as checked on October 19, 2026 and October 16, 2026. The prices are in different currencies, so convert before you compare. Use the true cost calculator for the full cost.
