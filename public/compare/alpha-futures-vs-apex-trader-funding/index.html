Alpha Futures vs Apex Trader Funding

Checked November 9, 2026 against both firms' own pages. Some links earn us a commission. Disclosure

Apex sells one-time evaluations with a choice of end-of-day or intraday drawdown, but did not show its current 50K prices when we checked. Both ban bots and full automation.

Which to pick

Pick Alpha Futures for no activation fee and up to 4 payouts a month, each after 5 winning days of $200 or more ($500 minimum, $3,000 maximum on 50K Standard). Pick Apex if you want no consistency rule in the evaluation. Its funded account pays $1,500 rising to $3,000 per request, but closes after six payouts.

Rules that trip people up

Alpha Futures' Standard plan has a 50% consistency rule in the evaluation and 40% once qualified. On Standard, Zero and Direct qualified accounts it bans orders from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after red-folder news. Apex allows news trading as part of a normal strategy. Alpha closes every position by 4:20 PM ET.

Questions traders ask

What is the difference between Alpha Futures and Apex Trader Funding drawdown?

Alpha Futures uses end-of-day trailing drawdown with a 50K max loss of $2,000 on its main plan. Apex Trader Funding uses a drawdown type that depends on the plan with a 50K max loss of $2,000 on its main plan. Figures as checked on November 9, 2026 and October 16, 2026.

Does Alpha Futures or Apex Trader Funding have a consistency rule?

Alpha Futures applies a consistency rule in the evaluation and on funded accounts. Apex Trader Funding applies a consistency rule on funded accounts only.

Do Alpha Futures and Apex Trader Funding accept US traders?

Yes. Both Alpha Futures and Apex Trader Funding accept US residents, according to their own pages (as checked on November 9, 2026 and October 16, 2026).
