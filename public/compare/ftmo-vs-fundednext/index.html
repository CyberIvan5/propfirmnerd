FTMO vs FundedNext

Checked October 16, 2026 against both firms' own pages.

Both sell a 50K two-step challenge with a static 10% max loss and a refundable fee. FTMO (€345) has the longer track record and a 4.8 Trustpilot score; FundedNext ($299.99, in US dollars) is likely cheaper depending on the exchange rate, also sells futures, but bans EAs on CFD accounts of $50K and above.

Which to pick

Pick FTMO for the longer track record and if you use EAs on a 50K account. Pick FundedNext if you want futures and CFDs from one firm or a lower price. FundedNext's 2-Step asks for 8% then 5%; FTMO's asks for 10% then 5%.
