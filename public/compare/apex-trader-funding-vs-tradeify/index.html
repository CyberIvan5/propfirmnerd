Apex Trader Funding vs Tradeify

Checked October 26, 2026 against both firms' own pages. Some links earn us a commission. Disclosure

Both charge one time for the evaluation. Tradeify has no activation fee, end-of-day drawdown on every plan and allows news trading; Apex lets you choose intraday or end-of-day drawdown and has no evaluation consistency rule, but caps each funded account at six payouts.

Which to pick

Tradeify is easier to model: one price, no activation fee, end-of-day drawdown. Apex suits traders who want no consistency target while passing and can work with the six-payout limit.

Questions traders ask

What is the difference between Apex Trader Funding and Tradeify drawdown?

Apex Trader Funding uses a drawdown type that depends on the plan with a 50K max loss of $2,000 on its main plan. Tradeify uses end-of-day trailing drawdown with a 50K max loss of $2,000 on its main plan. Figures as checked on October 16, 2026 and October 26, 2026.

Does Apex Trader Funding or Tradeify have a consistency rule?

Apex Trader Funding applies a consistency rule on funded accounts only. Tradeify applies a consistency rule on funded accounts only.

Do Apex Trader Funding and Tradeify accept US traders?

Yes. Both Apex Trader Funding and Tradeify accept US residents, according to their own pages (as checked on October 16, 2026 and October 26, 2026).
