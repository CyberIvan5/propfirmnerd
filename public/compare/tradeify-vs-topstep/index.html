Tradeify vs Topstep

Checked October 26, 2026 against both firms' own pages.

Tradeify charges once ($145 for a 50K Growth evaluation, with no activation fee). Topstep charges $49 a month plus a $149 activation fee on its Standard path. If you pass in the first month, Topstep's Standard path costs $198 in total against $145 at Tradeify. Both use a $2,000 end-of-day trailing drawdown on 50K and pay 90%.

Which to pick

Pick Tradeify if you want a single payment, trade news (it has no news rule), or want an instant funded account (Lightning Funded). Pick Topstep if you prefer a low monthly entry. It also offers a $95-a-month path with no activation fee.

Consistency rules

Tradeify's Growth evaluation has no consistency rule, but every funded payout request must pass a 35% rule. Its Select evaluation limits any day to 40% of total profit. Topstep's Combine limits your best day to 55% of the profit target and raises the target if you go over. Its Standard funded account has no consistency rule.
