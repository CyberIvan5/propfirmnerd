Lucid Trading vs Topstep

Checked November 2, 2026 against both firms' own pages. Some links earn us a commission. Disclosure

Lucid sells its 50K LucidFlex evaluation for a one-time $115 with no activation fee, and LucidFlex has no consistency rule once funded. Topstep costs $49 a month plus a $149 activation fee when you pass, or $95 a month with no activation fee, and publishes a route to a live account. Both use a $2,000 end-of-day trailing drawdown on 50K and pay 90%.

Which to pick

Pick Lucid if you want one payment and a short path to payouts. LucidFlex pays any day after 5 days of $150 or more, up to 50% of profit and $2,000 per request, and moves the account to live after 5 payouts. Pick Topstep if you expect to pass quickly and want the lowest entry price: one month on the Standard path plus activation costs $198.

Rules that trip people up

Topstep's Combine has a best-day limit of 55% of the profit target; going over raises the target instead of failing you. The LucidFlex evaluation uses a 40% rule. Lucid closes open positions at 4:45 PM ET, while Topstep wants you flat by 3:10 PM CT. Lucid allows bots and trade copiers but bans HFT; Topstep allows automated strategies only through its TopstepX API, and not on live accounts.
