FTMO vs The5ers

Checked October 16, 2026 against both firms' own pages. Some links earn us a commission. Disclosure

The5ers High Stakes is cheaper ($249 for 50K against €345 for FTMO's 2-Step) and asks for the same targets of 10% and then 5%. Its loss limits are tighter: 8% maximum and 4% daily, against 10% and 5% at FTMO. FTMO allows EAs within platform limits, while The5ers needs written approval for automated trading.

Which to pick

Pick FTMO if you want more room before a breach and plan to use EAs. Pick The5ers if price matters more and your risk per day stays well inside 4%. Both pay 80% at the start with a scaling plan that raises it. Both pay the first reward after 14 days.

News trading

Both restrict it on funded accounts in a similar way. FTMO bars order execution 2 minutes before and after selected high-impact news on its standard funded accounts. The5ers' High Stakes forbids new orders 2 minutes before and after high-impact news.

Questions traders ask

Is FTMO or The5ers cheaper?

FTMO's main 50K plan lists at €345 and The5ers' at $249, before discounts, as of October 16, 2026. The prices are in different currencies, so convert before you compare. Use the true cost calculator for the full cost.

What is the difference between FTMO and The5ers drawdown?

FTMO uses static drawdown with a 50K max loss of €5,000 on its main plan. The5ers uses static drawdown with a 50K max loss of $4,000 on its main plan. Figures as of October 16, 2026.
