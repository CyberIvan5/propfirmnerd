Before you buy an evaluation

An evaluation is a paid test. Most people who buy one do not pass, and the fee is gone when you fail. Funded accounts at most firms trade simulated money, and the firm pays you a share of simulated profits under its own rules. Firms also change rules with little notice, and some close. Read the risks and how prop firms make money before you spend anything.
