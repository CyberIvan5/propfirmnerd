Before you buy on a discount

A cheaper evaluation is still a paid test, and most people who buy one do not pass. A discount changes what a failed attempt costs you, not your odds of passing. Compare the rules first: the drawdown type and the max loss against the target matter more than 20% off the fee. Compare every firm or read the risks before you spend anything.

Codes normally cover the evaluation fee only. Resets, activation fees and monthly renewals are usually charged at the normal price, so the true cost of a monthly plan is higher than the first payment. The true cost calculator adds them up.

Questions traders ask

Which prop firm has the biggest Black Friday discount?

Phidias advertises up to 90% off with code BLACK90. Black Friday, first 1,000 accounts; ends Friday 27 November, 5 PM ET (100K Express to Live $135 instead of $900). A bigger percentage does not always mean a cheaper account: compare the final 50K price and the rules.

When do prop firm Black Friday deals end?

It depends on the firm. Phidias: November 27, 2026. Earn2Trade: about November 30, 2026. Alpha Futures: November 30, 2026. Aqua Funded: November 30, 2026. FXIFY: December 9, 2026. The others have not published an end date, so they can stop at any time.

Do Black Friday codes work on resets and activation fees?

Usually not. The codes cover the evaluation fee. Some firms waive or cut activation fees as part of a sale (Take Profit Trader's code waives its PRO activation fee), but check the checkout total before you pay.

Can I stack a Black Friday code with another code?

No firm we track lets you combine two discount codes on one order. Some add automatic bundle discounts when you buy several accounts at once.

Are these the real codes from the firms?
