Questions traders ask

What is the best forex prop firm?

We rank FTMO first. Running since 2015, static 10% max loss on the 2-Step and the fee refunded with the first reward. The5ers and FundedNext follow. The ranking weighs the drawdown rules, payout terms, track record and cost, so the right firm still depends on how you trade.

Which forex prop firms use static drawdown?

In this list, FTMO, The5ers, FundedNext, FundingPips, Blue Guardian, Goat Funded Trader, Alpha Capital Group, Aqua Funded, Blueberry Funded, BrightFunded, FunderPro, Funding Traders and Hola Prime use static drawdown on their main 50K plan. A static limit stays at a fixed level below your starting balance and does not follow your profits up.

Which forex prop firms have no consistency rule?

In this list, FTMO, FundedNext, FundingPips, Goat Funded Trader, Alpha Capital Group, Aqua Funded, Blueberry Funded, BrightFunded, FunderPro, Funding Traders and Hola Prime have no consistency rule on their main 50K plan, according to their own pages.
