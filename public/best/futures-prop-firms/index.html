Questions traders ask

What is the best futures prop firm?

We rank Tradeify first. One-time fee from $145, no activation fee, end-of-day drawdown on every plan, news trading allowed. Topstep and Lucid Trading follow. The ranking weighs how the 50K drawdown works, the payout rules and the cost, so the right firm still depends on how you trade.

What is the cheapest futures prop firm?

Among the 12 firms in this list, the lowest one-time 50K list price is Lucid Trading at $115 and the lowest monthly price is Topstep at $49 a month, before discounts. Activation and reset fees change the total, so check it with the true cost calculator.

Which futures prop firms use end-of-day drawdown?

In this list, Tradeify, Topstep, Lucid Trading, MyFundedFutures, Alpha Futures and Phidias use end-of-day trailing drawdown on their main 50K plan. At the others it is intraday trailing or depends on the plan you pick, so check the plan table in each review.
