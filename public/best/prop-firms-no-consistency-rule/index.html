Questions traders ask

Which prop firm does not have a consistency rule?

Among the firms we track, FTMO's 2-Step, Goat Funded Trader, Maven, BrightFunded, Blueberry Funded (Prime and 1-Step), FunderPro Classic and Funding Traders' 2-Step Pro10 have no consistency rule in the evaluation or on funded payouts. Apex, Tradeify Growth, MyFundedFutures, Phidias and Bulenox have none in the evaluation, but most of them add one before funded payouts.

Which prop firm has no consistency rule and no payout cap?

Futures firms usually cap each payout: for example $2,000 at Topstep (XFA Standard) and Lucid, and $1,500 to $3,000 at Apex and Tradeify Growth on 50K. Forex firms with no consistency rule, such as FTMO's 2-Step, Goat Funded Trader and BrightFunded, use payout cycles and minimums instead; we did not find a fixed per-payout dollar cap in their rules, but check the firm's payout page.

What is a consistency rule?

A limit on how much of your total profit can come from your best day, for example 40% or 50%. Break it in the evaluation and you either fail or have to keep trading until your other days catch up; break it on a funded account and the payout is usually delayed. See our consistency rule guide.
