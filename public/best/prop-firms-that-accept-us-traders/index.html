Futures or forex?

Futures prop firms such as Topstep, Apex, Tradeify, Lucid, MyFundedFutures, Alpha Futures, Phidias, TradeDay, Funded Futures Network and Earn2Trade all accept US residents. Their evaluations run on US futures platforms like Tradovate, NinjaTrader and Rithmic, so there is no platform catch.

Forex and CFD firms are different. Several of them say MetaTrader 5 is not available to US residents, so US traders get a smaller set of platforms:

  • FTMO: US clients are served through a separate US offering at ftmo.oanda.com.
  • E8 Markets: no MT5 for US citizens; US traders use TradeLocker, cTrader or Match-Trader through E8's US entity.
  • Goat Funded Trader: no MT5 or cTrader for US clients; TradeLocker, Match-Trader and Volumetrica are available.
  • Hola Prime: US clients can trade forex on Match-Trader and TradeLocker only.

Firms where US access is limited or unclear

These firms do not list the US as restricted, but limit what US residents can use, or do not say clearly: FunderPro (TradeLocker challenges only), Maven Trading (Match Trader only), BrightFunded (DXtrade only), The5ers (no MT5 for US residents) Funding Traders (MT5 not intended for US residents) and Alpha Capital Group (its help center says US residents can use DXtrade and TradeLocker, but its order form asks you to confirm you are not a US resident). For Aqua Funded, FundingPips, Blue Guardian, Bulenox, Take Profit Trader and Top One Futures, we could not find a clear statement either way. Ask the firm before you pay.

Firms that do not accept US residents

FXIFY and Blueberry Funded list the United States as a restricted country.

Questions traders ask

Can US residents use prop firms?

Yes. Most futures prop firms accept US residents, including Topstep, Apex, Tradeify, Lucid and MyFundedFutures. Many forex prop firms accept them too, but often not on MetaTrader 5.

Why can't US traders use MT5 at some prop firms?

Several forex prop firms, including E8, Goat Funded Trader, FunderPro and The5ers, say MetaTrader 5 is not available to US residents. They offer other platforms such as TradeLocker, Match-Trader, cTrader or DXtrade instead. The firms do not all give a reason.

Which prop firms do not accept US traders?

Among the firms we track, FXIFY and Blueberry Funded list the United States as restricted.

Is FTMO available in the US?

Yes, through a separate US offering run with OANDA at ftmo.oanda.com.
