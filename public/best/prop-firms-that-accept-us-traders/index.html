Prop firms that accept US traders

Updated October 16, 2026. Facts checked against each firm's own pages. How we rank

Every firm below either says on its own pages that it accepts US residents or leaves the US off its restricted-country list. Most are futures firms, which trade regulated US exchanges. Some forex firms serve US clients through a separate entity or a reduced set of platforms; the review for each firm says how.
