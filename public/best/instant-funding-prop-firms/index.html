Instant funding prop firms

Updated October 19, 2026. Facts checked against each firm's own pages. How we rank

Instant funding skips the evaluation: you pay more up front and trade a funded account from day one. The rules are usually stricter, with consistency requirements and lower payout caps. These firms sell an instant or direct account alongside their evaluations; open each review for the instant plan's terms.
