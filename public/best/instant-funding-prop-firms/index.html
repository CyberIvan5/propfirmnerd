Questions traders ask

Which instant funding prop firms skip the evaluation phase?

All firms on this page sell at least one account with no evaluation, including Tradeify (Lightning Funded), Alpha Futures (Direct), Funding Traders, Aqua Funded, Alpha Capital Group (Alpha Direct) and FTMO. The account is funded from the start, but you still have to meet payout conditions.

What is the catch with instant funding?

Stricter payout rules. For example, Tradeify's Lightning Funded applies a 20% consistency rule to the first payout and has a $1,000 minimum payout on 50K; Funding Traders' Instant account needs the balance 3.5% above the starting size and a 15% consistency score; Alpha Capital's Alpha Direct needs a 3% profit buffer and a 15% best-day rule; Aqua Funded's Instant Pro uses a 20% consistency rule.

Is instant funding worth it?

It costs more than an evaluation for the same account size, and the payout rules are tighter. It suits traders who are confident in their consistency and want to skip paying for a test first. Compare the total cost with the challenge cost calculator.
