What the price leaves out

  • Monthly billing. Topstep, Alpha Futures, TradeDay, Take Profit Trader and Top One Futures charge every month until you pass.
  • Activation fees. Topstep charges $149 per funded account on its Standard path, and Take Profit Trader $130 for a PRO account.
  • Bigger accounts, bigger limits. A 100K account usually doubles the profit target and raises the max loss, but not always in proportion. Check the rules for the size you buy in each review.

Questions traders ask

What is the cheapest 100K prop firm?

By list price in US dollars, the cheapest 100K account we track is Topstep: 100K Trading Combine for $99 a month; 150K for $199 a month. Monthly and one-time prices are ranked together, so check which kind you are buying.

How much does a 100K prop firm account cost?

Among the firms we track, 100K list prices run from $99 a month (Topstep) to $750 one-time (Funding Traders), before discount codes. A one-time 100K evaluation usually costs between 1.5 and 2 times the same firm's 50K price.

Is a 100K prop firm account worth it over 50K?

Only if the larger loss limit suits your trading. A 100K account gives a bigger max loss and usually higher payout caps, but the profit target rises too, and the fee is usually 1.5 to 2 times the 50K price. Compare the rules at each size in the firm's review.
