Updated November 9, 2026. Facts checked against each firm's own pages. How we rank. Some links earn us a commission. Disclosure

Questions traders ask

Which futures prop firms offer end-of-day trailing drawdown?

Among the firms we track: Topstep, Tradeify, Lucid, Alpha Futures, Funded Futures Network, MyFundedFutures (Builder, Rapid EOD and Pro), Phidias (Fundamental and Premium), Top One Futures, Hola Prime futures and Bulenox (end-of-day option). Apex and TradeDay let you choose end-of-day or intraday.

Which futures prop firms have intraday trailing drawdown?

Apex sells an intraday trailing evaluation and performance account alongside its end-of-day one, and TradeDay lets you choose intraday in the evaluation. Take Profit Trader uses end-of-day in the Test but intraday on PRO accounts, MyFundedFutures' Rapid plan switches to intraday once funded, and Earn2Trade uses intraday trailing on Live accounts.

Which prop firm has the lowest drawdown?

The smallest 50K max loss we track is Phidias' Express to Live account: $650, static. Maven's Standard 3 Step allows 3% ($1,500), also static. A smaller drawdown is cheaper or faster to pass but leaves very little room; see the biggest-drawdown list for the other end.
