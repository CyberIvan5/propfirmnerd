Questions traders ask

What is the best prop firm for beginners?

We put FTMO first for beginners. Static max loss on the 2-Step, no time limit and the fee back with your first reward. Tradeify and Topstep follow.

What should a beginner look for in a prop firm?

A loss limit that does not trail intraday (static or end-of-day drawdown), a max loss that is large next to the profit target, rules written out clearly on the firm's own site, and a low cost to try again. Be careful with plans where a consistency rule pushes you to trade bigger than you planned.

How much money do I need to start with a prop firm?

You pay the evaluation fee, not the account size. In this list the lowest 50K list prices in US dollars are Lucid Trading at $136 (one-time) and Topstep at $49 a month (monthly), before discounts. Most traders do not pass on the first attempt, so budget for more than one.
